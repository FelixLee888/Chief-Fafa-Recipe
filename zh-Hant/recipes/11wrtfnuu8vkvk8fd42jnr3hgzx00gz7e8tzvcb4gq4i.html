材料

  • 豬腩肉去皮
  • 香茅段
  • 4塊南乳
  • 50ml清酒
  • 2湯匙花生醬
  • 1茶匙糖
  • 1粒雞蛋
  • 2茶匙木薑子油
  • 半茶匙黃薑粉
  • 適量雞精
  • 1茶匙鹽
  • 檸檬葉
  • 200ml伏特加
  • 砂糖
  • 調醃料 4塊

做法

  1. Marinate: Pour the marinade over the pork belly, add the lemongrass sections, and mix well.
  2. Signature "Perfume" Sauce: Place lemongrass sections and kaffir lime leaves into a jar, pour in 200ml伏特加, and refrigerate to infuse for at least 1 hour.
  3. Roast: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F) and roast the pork belly for 1 hour.
