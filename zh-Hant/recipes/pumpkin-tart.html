準備: 10 min
烹調: 55 min
總計: 1 hr 5 min
份量: TBD

材料

  • 125g unsalted butter, softened
  • 80g icing sugar
  • 30g ground almonds
  • 50g beaten egg
  • 250g plain flour
  • 2g salt
  • 300g good quality pumpkin purée
  • 100g double cream
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 100g whole egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 15g cornflour
  • Vanilla, to taste
  • Ground cinnamon, ginger and nutmeg, to taste

做法

  1. Mix in the ground almonds and egg.
  2. Add the flour and salt, mixing just until a dough forms.
  3. Bake at 160°C for 20-25 minutes until golden.
  4. Whisk pumpkin purée, double cream, brown sugar, whole egg, egg yolk, cornflour, vanilla, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and salt until smooth.
  5. Bake at 150°C until the edges are set and the centre has a slight wobble, approximately 20-40 minutes depending on depth.
