味噌米粒義大利麵

晚餐五分：味噌米粒義大利麵 5 種食材（4 份）： 1. 450 克米粒 2.85 克奶油（無鹽） 3. 45 克白味噌醬 4. 115 克帕瑪森起司，細碎 5. 80 克香菇 柔軟度： 1. 去除蘑菇，又切成中等大小的奶油片。 3.同時煮義大利麵，煮好後備用400毫升義大利麵水。 4. 將味噌和 250 毫升義大利麵水（約一半）加入奶油和蘑菇中，攪拌至混合。 5. 加入帕瑪森起司和義大利麵，攪拌至絲滑細膩。真的給它一些苦勞！

材料

  • 450g Orzo
  • 85g Unsalted Butter
  • 45g White miso paste
  • 115g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 80g Shiitake mushrooms
  • 115g Parmesan Cheese
  • 400ml 預留義大利麵水

做法

  1. Chop the mushrooms into medium-sized slices.
  2. Melt the butter and fry the mushrooms until golden but still soft.
  3. Boil the orzo pasta and reserve 400ml of the pasta water when cooked.
  4. Whisk the miso paste with about 250ml of the reserved pasta water and add to the butter and mushrooms;
  5. stir until combined.
  6. Add the Parmesan and the cooked pasta;
  7. whisk until silky and creamy.
  8. Chop mushrooms into medium-sized slices.
  9. Boil orzo pasta, reserving 400ml of the cooking water.
  10. Mix miso with 250ml of the reserved pasta water and combine with the butter and mushrooms.
  11. Add grated Parmesan and cooked pasta, stirring until creamy.
  12. Remove mushroom stalks and chop for medium sized slices
  13. 將奶油融化，煎至金黃色但仍柔軟。
  14. At the same time, boil pasta and reserve 400ml pasta water once cooked.
  15. Add miso and 250ml of the pasta water (around half of it) to the butter and mushrooms and whisk until combined.
  16. Add parmesan, and pasta and whisk until silky and creamy. Really give it some elbow grease! If it needs thinning, add some more pasta water until you get the consistency you like.
