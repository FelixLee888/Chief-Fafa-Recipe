Instructions

  1. Mix all-purpose flour, flour, water and salt evenly.
  2. Add the marinade to the pork, add water (20 ml) in portions and stir in the same direction until the water is completely absorbed.
  3. Steam it in a steamer for 8-10 minutes, or fry it in a pan until the bottom is golden brown, then cover the pan with water and cook.
  4. Mix all-purpose flour, cooked noodles, water and salt evenly.
  5. Add the pork to the seasoning and add water in portions to fully absorb.
  6. Put the wrapped dumplings into boiling water and cook for 8-10 minutes, or steam them in a pot until the bottom is golden brown, add water and cook until cooked through.
  7. Stir all-purpose flour, flour, water and salt evenly. Knead with hands for 2 minutes and wrap the baking powder in plastic wrap for 5 minutes. Repeat three times (a total of 15 minutes of resting) until the dough is smooth, stretchable and elastic.
  8. Add the marinade to the pork, add water (20 ml) in portions and stir in the same direction until the water is completely absorbed. The filling has enough moisture so that the result will be tender and juicy (the specific amount depends on the water absorption of the pork).
  9. Divide the dough into ten equal parts and roll them out, then fill them with crab meat filling. Steam it in a steamer for 8-10 minutes, or fry it in a pan until the bottom is golden brown, then cover the pan with water and cook. )
