Crab Roe Pork Dumplings

Filled with crab paste and marinated pork, wrapped in homemade dumpling wrappers, it can be steamed or pan-fried. The filling absorbs moisture in batches, making it tender and juicy.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 45 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Pork 250g
  • Appropriate amount of crab paste (crab roe)
  • All-purpose flour 180g
  • 20 grams of clear noodles
  • water 100ml
  • Salt 1/8 teaspoon
  • Soy sauce 1 tablespoon
  • sugar 1 teaspoon
  • sesame oil 2 tsp
  • wine 1 tablespoon
  • pepper a little
  • Chicken powder half teaspoon
  • water 20ml
  • Corn flour 2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Mix all-purpose flour, flour, water and salt evenly and knead with hands for 2 minutes.
  2. Wrap the dough with plastic wrap and let it rest for 5 minutes; repeat kneading and resting three times for a total of 15 minutes, until the dough is smooth, stretchable and elastic.
  3. Add soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, wine, pepper, chicken powder and corn flour to the pork and mix well.
  4. Add 20 ml of water to the pork filling one at a time and stir in the same direction until the water is completely absorbed.
  5. Divide the dough into ten equal parts, roll them out one by one, and fill them with pork filling and crab paste (crab roe).
  6. Steaming: Place the dumplings in a steamer basket and steam for 8 to 10 minutes.
  7. Frying: Fry the dumplings until the bottom is golden brown, add water, cover the pot, and fry until cooked through.

Notes

  • The actual amount of water added to the pork filling can be fine-tuned according to the degree of water absorption of the pork.
  • The filling fully absorbs moisture, helping the finished product stay fresh and juicy.
  • Dumplings can be steamed or fried.
