Prep: 10 min
Cook: 1 hr
Total: 2 hr 10 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Pork belly skinless
  • lemongrass section
  • 4 pieces of southern milk
  • 50ml sake
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 teaspoons ginger seed oil
  • half teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Appropriate amount of chicken essence
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • lemon leaves
  • 200ml vodka
  • granulated sugar
  • 4 pieces of marinade

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Pour the marinade over the pork belly, add the lemongrass sections, and mix well.
  2. Signature "Perfume" Sauce: Place lemongrass sections and kaffir lime leaves into a jar, pour in 200ml vodka, and refrigerate to infuse for at least 1 hour.
  3. Roast: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F) and roast the pork belly for 1 hour.
