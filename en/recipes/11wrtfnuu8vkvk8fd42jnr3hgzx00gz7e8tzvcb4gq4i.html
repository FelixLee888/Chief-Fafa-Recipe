Lemongrass Crispy BBQ Pork

Pork belly is marinated in southern milk, peanut butter, sake and spices, then rolled in sugar and grilled until caramelized and crispy. Serve with lemongrass perfume infused with lemongrass, lemon leaves and vodka.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 hr
Total: 3 hr 15 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Skinless Pork Belly
  • lemongrass section
  • 4 pieces of southern milk
  • 50ml sake
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 teaspoons ginger seed oil
  • half teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Appropriate amount of chicken essence
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • lemon leaves
  • 200ml vodka
  • sugar

Instructions

  1. Peel the pork belly and cut the lemongrass into sections.
  2. Put the southern milk, sake, peanut butter, sugar, eggs, jellyfish oil, turmeric powder, chicken essence and salt into a blender and mix well to form a marinade.
  3. Pour the marinade onto the pork belly, add the lemongrass segments, mix well, and marinate for 1 hour.
  4. Put lemongrass segments and lemon leaves into a storage bottle, pour in 200ml vodka, refrigerate and soak for at least 1 hour.
  5. Remove the marinade from the surface of the pork belly, add plenty of sugar and mix well; remove excess marinade and sugar from the surface before putting it in the oven.
  6. Preheat the oven to 190°C, add the pork belly and roast for 1 hour.
  7. Spray a small amount of infused lemongrass vodka perfume when eating.

Notes

  • Lemongrass vodka perfume needs to be refrigerated and soaked for at least 1 hour before use.
  • Before putting the pork belly into the oven, remove excess marinade from the surface to allow the sugar to form a crispy skin.
