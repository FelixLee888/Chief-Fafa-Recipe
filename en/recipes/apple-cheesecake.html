Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F), top and bottom heat.
  2. Mix the melted butter, sugar, vanilla, eggs, and milk.
  3. Stir in the all-purpose flour, baking powder, and salt until just combined, then fold in the diced apple.
  4. Mix the quark or unsalted cream cheese, sugar, egg yolk, cornstarch, and vanilla until smooth.
  5. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 25–30 minutes, until golden brown.
  6. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F), top and bottom heat. Line a 6-cup muffin pan with paper liners.
  7. Mix the melted butter, sugar, vanilla, eggs, and milk. Stir in the all-purpose flour, baking powder, and salt until just combined, then fold in the diced apple.
  8. Mix the quark or unsalted cream cheese, sugar, egg yolk, cornstarch, and vanilla until smooth. Spoon a generous dollop onto each muffin.
