Instructions

  1. Add the tapioca starch, sugar, and matcha powder to a saucepan and mix.
  2. Add water and mix well.
  3. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly from the bottom, until the mixture becomes translucent.
  4. Transfer the mixture to cold water and shape it into bite-sized pieces.
  5. Mix the kinako, powdered sugar, matcha powder, and salt together.
