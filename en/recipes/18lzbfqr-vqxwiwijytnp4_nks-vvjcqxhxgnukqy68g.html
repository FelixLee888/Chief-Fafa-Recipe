Shredded Pork Fried Noodles

The noodles are first boiled, seasoned with light soy sauce, and then slowly fried in an appropriate amount of oil until golden brown on both sides, keeping them crispy on the outside and soft on the inside. Pork neck meat is served with gravy made from chicken stock and corn flour, and finally the dough is placed on top of the toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pork neck
  • light soy sauce
  • chicken soup
  • Corn flour

Instructions

  1. Boil the noodles.
  2. Season the noodles with light soy sauce.
  3. Slowly fry the noodles in an appropriate amount of oil until the surface is crispy and both sides are golden. Avoid frying the noodles in large amounts.
  4. Use pork neck instead of lean meat and fry the meat and other ingredients well.
  5. First mix the chicken broth and corn flour, then immediately pour into the fried ingredients.
  6. Slowly cook the gravy until it boils.
  7. Place the cooked ingredients on the plate, then put the fried noodles on top of the ingredients and eat.

Notes

  • Pork neck meat is less likely to be fried until it becomes dry and hard than lean meat.
  • The chicken soup should be mixed with cornstarch first, then immediately pour in the fried ingredients to prevent the gravy from clumping.
  • Put the noodles on top of the toppings last to prevent the gravy from turning the golden noodles into chewy.
