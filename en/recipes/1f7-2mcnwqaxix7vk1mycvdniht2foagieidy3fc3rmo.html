Figs braised in red wine

It is a sweet that is made by boiling figs with red wine and granulated sugar and then cooling them. Remove from heat and cool in the refrigerator.

Ingredients

  • 400g figs
  • 1 bottle of red wine (750g)
  • 200g sugar

Instructions

  1. Put red wine and granulated sugar in a pot and heat.
  2. Once it boils, heat it over medium heat for 3 minutes.
  3. Add figs and cook over medium heat for 10 minutes.
  4. Cover with a lid so that the figs are submerged in the broth, and cook over medium heat for another 10 minutes.
  5. Let it cool, and once it has cooled down, transfer it to a container and chill it in the refrigerator.

Notes

  • Red wine is replaced with the same amount of grape juice.
  • When using grape juice, reduce the granulated sugar by 20-30% compared to when using red wine, as it has a sweeter taste.
