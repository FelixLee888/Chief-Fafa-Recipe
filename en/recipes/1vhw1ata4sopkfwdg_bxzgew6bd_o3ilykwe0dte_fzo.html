Prep: 10 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 30 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter 112.5g
  • 85g powdered sugar
  • pinch of salt
  • 50g whole eggs
  • Almond powder 30g
  • 225g soft flour
  • 1 piece of skin
  • 60g powdered sugar
  • 5g water
  • 8g fruit juice
  • Chopped pistachios to taste

Instructions

  1. ① Smooth the butter that has been brought back to room temperature, add powdered sugar and salt, and mix.
  2. ② Add eggs in 3 parts and mix well each time.
  3. ③ Add sifted almond powder and soft flour and mix gently.
  4. Add the grated lemon zest and mix until there are no lumps.
  5. ⑦ Cut the dough into 2cm wide pieces and bake in a preheated oven at 170℃ for 15-18 minutes until golden brown.
  6. ⑧ Add water and lemon juice to the powdered sugar and mix until smooth.
