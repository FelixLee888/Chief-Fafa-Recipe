lemon cookies

The cookies are baked with lemon zest and finished with lemon-flavored grass arrows and chopped pistachios.

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 40 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter 112.5g
  • 145g powdered sugar
  • pinch of salt
  • 50g whole eggs
  • Almond powder 30g
  • 225g soft flour
  • 1 piece of skin
  • 5g water
  • 8g fruit juice
  • Chopped pistachios to taste

Instructions

  1. Smooth the unsalted butter that has been brought back to room temperature, add 85g of powdered sugar and salt, and mix.
  2. Add the whole eggs in 3 parts and mix well each time.
  3. Add the sifted almond powder and soft flour and mix gently. Add the grated lemon zest and mix until there are no lumps.
  4. Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.
  5. Divide the dough into 2 parts, roll each part into a thin strip, and then divide into 2 equal parts. Place each piece between pieces of baking paper and fold in the four sides to form a rectangle. Turn it over and use a rolling pin to roll it out to the corners.
  6. Let the rolled out dough rest in the refrigerator for 1 hour.
  7. Cut the dough into 2cm wide pieces and bake in a preheated oven at 170℃ for 15-18 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Add water and lemon juice to 60g of powdered sugar and mix until smooth to make glass arrows.
  9. Dip the cooled cookies into a glass arrow and garnish with chopped pistachios. Once the icing is dry, it's done.

Notes

  • Let the dough rest for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.
  • After rolling out the dough, let it rest in the refrigerator for 1 hour.
  • Once the finishing icing has dried, it's complete.
