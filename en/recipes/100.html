100% rice flour bread

A gluten-free loaf of bread made with 100% rice flour for confectionery. Select rice flour, mix the ingredients to make dough, and let it rise in a bread mold.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Natural dry yeast 7g
  • 40g hot water
  • Rice flour for confectionery (KOKU "Konadamon") 350g
  • 24g sugar
  • 7g sea salt
  • 280g hot water
  • 28g vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Activate natural dry yeast with 40g of lukewarm water.
  2. Mix confectionery rice flour, cane sugar, and sea salt.
  3. Add activated yeast water and 280g of lukewarm water and form a dough. Adjust the amount of lukewarm water depending on the condition of the dough.
  4. Add 28g of vegetable oil and knead.
  5. Pour the dough into a bread mold and let it rise.

Notes

  • It is important to choose high-quality rice flour that has minimal starch damage.
  • The rice flour used is KOKU ``Konadamon'' rice flour for confectionery.
  • Use a bread mold for 1 loaf.
