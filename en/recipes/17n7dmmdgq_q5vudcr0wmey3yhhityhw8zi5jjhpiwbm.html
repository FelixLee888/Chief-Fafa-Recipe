Prep: 30 min
Cook: 1 hr 30 min
Total: 2 hr
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Approximately 750g
  • Edible baking soda...1 tablespoon
  • Sugar: half the amount of peeled chestnuts

Instructions

  1. Wash the chestnuts, wipe them dry, put them in a storage bag, and freeze them in the freezer for at least 1 day.
  2. Place the chestnuts from ① in a bowl and pour boiling water over them for 15 minutes.
  3. Put ③ into a pot, add plenty of water and edible baking soda, heat, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Gently drain ④ into a colander and place in a bowl of water and gently wash to remove any streaks.
  5. Put ⑤ and water in a pot, heat, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low for 15 minutes.
  6. Weigh the chestnuts, add 1/2 of the amount of sugar, add 1/2 cup of water, bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.Turn off the heat, leave the pot in the pot and let it cool until cool. Add the remaining sugar, put a lid on it, bring it to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes, turn off the heat, leave the pot in the pot, let it cool down, taste it, and if it's not sweet enough, add about 1 tablespoon, drop the lid again, put it on the fire, bring it to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
  7. After cooling thoroughly, place the chestnuts in a boiling storage bottle, fill the bottle with syrup to the very limit, cover and store in the refrigerator.
  8. ① Wash the chestnuts, wipe them dry and put them in a storage bag.
  9. ②Put the chestnuts from ① in a bowl and pour boiling water over them for 15 minutes.
  10. ④Put ③ into a pot, add plenty of water and edible baking soda, heat, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low for 15 minutes.
  11. ⑤ Gently drain ④ into a colander and place in a bowl of water and gently wash to remove any streaks.
  12. ⑥ Put ⑤ and water in a pot, heat, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low for 15 minutes.
  13. ⑧Weigh the chestnuts, add 1/2 of the amount of sugar, add 1/2 cup of water, heat, bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
  14. Add the remaining sugar, cover with a lid, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes, turn off the heat, leave the pot to cool, then taste and if it's not sweet enough, add about 1 tablespoon, drop the lid again, bring to the boil, bring to a boil, then reduce to a low heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
  15. ⑨After cooling thoroughly, put the chestnuts in a boiled storage bottle and add syrup to the top of the bottle.
