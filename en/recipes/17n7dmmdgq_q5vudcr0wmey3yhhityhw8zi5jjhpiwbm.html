Boiled chestnuts with astringent skin

Chestnuts are boiled with astringent skin, which is made by freezing the chestnuts, peeling them, and removing the scum with baking soda. Add sugar in portions and boil until cool, then store.

Ingredients

  • Approximately 750g
  • Edible baking soda: 1 tablespoon
  • Sugar: half the amount of peeled chestnuts

Instructions

  1. Wash and dry the chestnuts, place them in a storage bag, and freeze them in the freezer for at least 1 day.
  2. Place the frozen chestnuts in a bowl, cover with boiling water, and let stand for 15 minutes.
  3. Peel off the skin so as not to damage the astringent skin.
  4. Place chestnuts, plenty of water, and edible baking soda in a pot and heat. Once it boils, lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Gently drain the chestnuts in a colander, place them in a bowl of water, and wash them gently to remove any streaks.
  6. Put the chestnuts and water in a pot, bring to a boil, then simmer over low heat for 15 minutes. Once done, put it in water and wash it again.
  7. Weigh the peeled chestnuts and add half of the sugar. Add enough water to cover the chestnuts, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave the pot to cool.
  8. Once cool, add the remaining sugar, cover, and heat. Once it boils, reduce the heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes, then turn off the heat and leave the pot to cool. After it cools down, taste it, and if it's not sweet enough, add about 1 tablespoon of sugar, drop the lid on it again, bring it to a boil, then simmer over low heat for 15 minutes.
  9. After cooling thoroughly, place the chestnuts in a boiling storage jar. Pour the syrup into the bottle, cover it, and store it in the refrigerator.

Notes

  • Freeze the chestnuts in the freezer for at least a day, then soak them in boiling water to begin the peeling process.
  • Boil the storage jar before using it, and let the chestnuts and syrup cool sufficiently before packing.
  • Store in the refrigerator.
