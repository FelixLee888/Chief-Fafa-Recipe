Vegan Jasmine White Peach Jelly

This vegan jasmine white peach jelly can make about 2 cups (each cup is about 280ml) and consists of candied white peaches, white peach jelly, jasmine soy milk and jasmine fresh cream. Candied white peaches can also be eaten alone or used in cakes.
