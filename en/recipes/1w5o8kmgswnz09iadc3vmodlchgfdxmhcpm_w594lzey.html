Baked apple cheesecake

A baked cheesecake made using heated red apples, made in a round mold with a diameter of 12cm. Use biscuit dough and almonds and watch the baking until finished.

Cook: TBD

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Approximately 200g
  • 15g sugar
  • Biscuit (choice) 80g
  • 30g unsalted butter
  • Fresh cream (47%) 30g
  • 20g unsalted butter
  • 30g sugar
  • 12g honey
  • 40g sliced almonds

Instructions

  1. Line the mold with parchment paper.
  2. Heat the apples and remove from heat.
  3. Drain the apples well and place them in a mold.
  4. Bake in the oven and keep an eye on it as it starts to brown and adjust accordingly.

Notes

  • Drain apples thoroughly before using.
  • Remove from heat, drain, and place in a mold.
  • When it starts to brown, check and adjust the color.
