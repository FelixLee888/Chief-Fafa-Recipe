Rich black sesame basque cheesecake

A rich and smooth Basque cheesecake with the richness of black sesame and white chocolate. You can enjoy it like a Japanese sweet by adding whipped kinako and brown sugar syrup.

Ingredients

  • cream cheese 200g
  • 50g sugar
  • 40g white chocolate
  • 60g ground black sesame
  • 2 eggs (M size)
  • Fresh cream 180g
  • Approximately 10g
  • 1 teaspoon white rum
  • [Kinako whip] Fresh cream 100ml
  • [Kinako whip] 1 tablespoon soybean
  • 7g sugar
  • [Finish] Appropriate amount of commercially available brown sugar syrup
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 120ml water

Instructions

  1. Bring cream cheese and eggs to room temperature. Melt the white chocolate in a double boiler or in the microwave (600W for about 30 seconds). Preheat the oven to 240℃.
  2. Add cream cheese and sugar to a bowl and mix thoroughly until smooth.
  3. Add the black sesame paste to the melted white chocolate, mix, and add to the cream cheese bowl. It's okay if it looks like it's separated, so mix it until it's evenly mixed.
  4. Beat the eggs and add in about 3 times. Mix thoroughly with a hand mixer each time.
  5. Add fresh cream, rice flour, and white rum and mix until smooth. Rub the dough.
  6. Wet a cookie sheet with water, crumple it up, spread it out, and wipe it dry. Place in a 12cm round mold and pour in the batter.
  7. Reduce the oven temperature to 230℃ and bake for 19-20 minutes. When you shake the mold, the aim is for the center to shake completely.
  8. Once baked, remove from heat and chill in the refrigerator overnight or for at least 4 hours.
  9. To make soybean whip, put the fresh cream, soybean flour, and cane sugar in a chilled bowl and whisk until stiff peaks form.
  10. Garnish with whipped mushrooms of your choice and drizzle with brown sugar syrup.
  11. To make homemade brown sugar syrup, put brown sugar and water in a pot and boil over low heat until it thickens slightly. Let cool before using.

Notes

  • Before baking, preheat the oven to 240℃, add the dough, and lower the temperature to 230℃.
  • When baked, the center should be bubbly and jiggling.
  • Chill the baked cake in the refrigerator overnight, or at least 4 hours.
  • Add whipped kinako and brown sugar syrup to taste.
