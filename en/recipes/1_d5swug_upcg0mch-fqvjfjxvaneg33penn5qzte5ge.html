Ingredients

  • Cream cheese…200g
  • Cane sugar…50g
  • White chocolate…40g
  • Kneaded black sesame seeds…60g
  • Eggs (M size)…2 pieces
  • Fresh cream…180g
  • Approximately 10g
  • White rum…1 teaspoon
  • Fresh cream for soybean whipped cream…100ml
  • Kinako...1 tablespoon
  • Cane sugar…7g
  • Commercially available brown sugar syrup…appropriate amount
  • Brown sugar…100g
  • Water…120ml

Instructions

  1. Preparation: Melt the white chocolate in a double boiler or in the microwave (600w for about 30 seconds).
  2. Preheat the oven to 240℃.
  3. Add cream cheese and sugar to a bowl and mix thoroughly until smooth.
  4. 溶かしたホワイトチョコレートに練り黒ごまを加えて混ぜ、①の生地と合わせてムラがなくなるまで混ぜる（分離しても大丈夫）。
  5. 卵を溶き、3回程度に分けて加え、その都度ハンドミキサーでしっかり混ぜる。
  6. 生クリーム・米粉・ホワイトラムを加え、全体がなめらかになるまで混ぜる。
  7. Wet a parchment paper with water, roll it up, place it in a mold, and pour the batter into the mold.
  8. Reduce the oven temperature to 230℃ and bake for 19-20 minutes.
  9. Once baked, remove from heat and chill thoroughly in the refrigerator overnight (at least 4 hours).
  10. Put the fresh cream, soybean flour, and cane sugar into a chilled bowl and whisk until stiff peaks form.
