Perfect Choux au Craquelin

Choux pastry is topped with a thin, chilled craquelin layer for a crisp finish. Prepare and chill the craquelin before making the choux pastry.

Prep: 20 min
Cook: the mixture over medium heat
Total: 1 hr 55 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 100 grams flour
  • 100 grams sugar
  • 80 grams butter
  • 100 grams water
  • 100 grams milk
  • 5 grams sugar
  • 5 grams salt
  • 100 grams butter
  • 120 grams flour
  • 200 grams eggs

Instructions

  1. Mix 100 grams of flour, 100 grams of sugar, and 80 grams of butter to form the craquelin dough.
  2. Knead the dough until it is even and firm.
  3. Place the dough between two silicone sheets and roll it to about 3 to 4 millimeters thick.
  4. Set the rolled dough in the refrigerator for 1 to 2 hours.
  5. Cut the chilled dough into rounds with a round cutter and keep the rounds refrigerated.
  6. Combine the water, milk, sugar, salt, and 100 grams of butter, then heat until the butter has melted.
  7. Add 120 grams of flour while stirring.
  8. Continue stirring and cook the mixture over medium heat.
  9. Remove the mixture from the heat and let it cool for 5 minutes.
  10. Add the eggs to the cooled mixture.
  11. Use the chilled craquelin rounds with the choux pastry before baking.

Notes

  • The craquelin dough is very soft, so roll it between two sheets.
  • Keep the cut craquelin rounds refrigerated until needed.
  • The craquelin can turn soft quickly if it is not baked.
