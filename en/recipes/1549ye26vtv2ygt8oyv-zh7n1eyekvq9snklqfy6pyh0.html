black sesame pudding

A black sesame pudding that can be made just by mixing without using an oven. By carefully and thoroughly cooking the eggs, the richness comes out.

Prep: TBD
Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 5g powdered gelatin
  • 30g water
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 30g sugar
  • 200g milk
  • white chocolate 40g
  • Sesame paste 90g
  • Fresh cream 170g
  • (optional) 100g fresh cream
  • 6g sugar

Instructions

  1. Let the eggs come to room temperature.
  2. Add gelatin powder to 30g of cold water, mix gently, and leave to soften in the refrigerator for about 10 minutes.
  3. Mix egg yolks, granulated sugar, milk, white chocolate, sesame paste, and fresh cream.
  4. Heat the eggs carefully and thoroughly.
  5. Add softened gelatin and mix.
  6. Divide into containers and cool to solidify.

Notes

  • Capacity is 5 to 6 cups.
  • This is a recipe that does not require an oven.
  • Add powdered gelatin to cold water and mix gently to soften.
