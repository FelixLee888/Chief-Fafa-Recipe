Instructions

  1. Put the grapefruit peel into a container, add salt and mix well, pour in water that has not covered the peel, seal with plastic wrap, refrigerate and soak overnight to remove the bitterness to the greatest extent.
  2. Cut into shreds + remove the bitterness again - take out the soaked grapefruit peel, rinse it with water, cut it into thin strips, rinse it again and drain it.
  3. Cook the basic sauce - add an appropriate amount of purified water to the pot, add shredded grapefruit peels, cook over high heat until the shredded peels become transparent, add rock sugar and stir until melted.
  4. Add the pulp and cook until thickened - tear the peeled grapefruit pulp into small pieces, pour it into the pot, turn to medium to low heat and simmer slowly, stirring constantly to avoid burning the bottom, simmer until it is 9 minutes dry (the sauce is thick and becomes amber in color) and then turn off the heat.
  5. Bottle and store - Boil, sterilize and dry the glass jar in advance, put the grapefruit tea sauce into the jar while it is hot, cover it tightly and invert it to create a vacuum, let it cool and then refrigerate.
