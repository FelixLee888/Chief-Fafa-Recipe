Earl Grey roasted sweet persimmon

Roasted sweet persimmon with Earl Gray tea, sugar, and lemon, caramelised until soft and fragrant.

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 1 sweet persimmon (rich sweet persimmons are recommended)
  • 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/8 teaspoon Earl Gray tea powder

Instructions

  1. Peel the persimmons and cut into pieces.
  2. Add sugar, lemon juice and Earl Gray tea powder and mix gently.
  3. Place in the oven and bake at 190°C for 25 minutes.
