LavenderBiscuits

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 25 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 175g unsalted butter, softened
  • tbsp fresh lavender flowers and leaves, finely chopped (or 1tbsp
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 225g plain flour
  • 25g demerara sugar (for rolling)

Instructions

  1. Cream the butter with the fresh lavender, beat in the caster sugar, then stir in the flour to make a soft dough.
  2. Slice into 20 biscuits.
  3. Bake at 160°C fan (180°C/325°F/Gas 3) for around 15 minutes, until just pale golden at the edges.
