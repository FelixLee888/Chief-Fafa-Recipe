Salted egg yolk cheese madeleine

The savory salted egg yolk cheese filling is paired with maple madeleine to balance the sweetness. Makes about 15 to 16 pieces.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 25 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Cake powder 120g
  • Aluminum-free baking powder 5g
  • Unsalted butter 95g
  • Whole eggs 100g
  • 60g sugar
  • Maple syrup 30g
  • Cream cheese 25g
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • Fresh animals 15g
  • Maple syrup 10g
  • Unsalted butter 20g
  • Golden Cheese Powder 2g
  • 2 to 3 salted egg yolks

Instructions

  1. Melt 95g of unsalted butter for the Madeleine batter and set aside.
  2. Sift the flour and aluminum-free baking powder.
  3. Mix whole eggs, sugar and maple syrup until evenly combined.
  4. Add the sifted flour and mix well.
  5. Slowly add the melted cream and mix well.
  6. Refrigerate the batter overnight.
  7. Mix the cream cheese, cheese slices, fresh vegetables, maple syrup, unsalted butter, golden cheese powder and salted egg yolk evenly to make the filling.
  8. Bake at 200/200 for 8 to 10 minutes and serve.

Notes

  • This batter will make about 15 to 16 madeleines.
  • The amount of filling will be larger.
  • It is not recommended to replace the white sugar, but the original text states that replacement will not affect the production.
