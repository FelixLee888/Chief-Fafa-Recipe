Ingredients

  • 400 g puff pastry, from the refrigerated section
  • ml milk ( 1cup
  • 1 egg yolk
  • g sugar ( 3tbsp
  • g cornstarch ( 2tbsp
  • some freshly ground vanilla (from a vanilla mill)
  • For brushing: 1 egg yolk
  • For brushing: 1 tsp milk
  • sliced almonds
  • powdered sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. Heat the milk with the vanilla.
  2. Whisk together the egg yolk, sugar, and cornstarch.
  3. Gradually whisk in the warm milk.
  4. Return everything to the saucepan and cook while stirring until a thick custard forms.
  5. Cover directly with plastic wrap and let cool completely.
  6. Bake at 200°C (390°F), top and bottom heat, for about 18–22 minutes until golden brown and flaky.
  7. Whisk together the egg yolk, sugar, and cornstarch. Gradually whisk in the warm milk.
  8. Return everything to the saucepan and cook while stirring until a thick custard forms. Cover directly with plastic wrap and let cool completely.
