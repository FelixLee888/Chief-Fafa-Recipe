The ultimate smooth and rich matcha basque cheesecake

A smooth and rich Basque cheesecake made with matcha, cream cheese, and mascarpone. It is made in a 15cm mold with a removable bottom, and after baking it is cooled overnight.

Ingredients

  • For a 15cm mold with a removable bottom: 300g cream cheese
  • Matcha 23g
  • 100g sugar
  • For a 15cm mold with a removable bottom: 100g mascarpone
  • 12g rice flour
  • For a 15cm mold with a removable bottom: 200g fresh cream
  • For 12cm round: 170g cream cheese
  • Matcha 13g
  • Sugar 57g
  • For 12cm round shape: Mascarpone 57g
  • Whole egg 102g
  • 7g rice flour
  • For 12cm round shape: 114g of fresh cream

Instructions

  1. Before making the dough, wet the parchment paper with water to make it soft, wipe it dry, and then spread it into a 15cm mold.
  2. Soften 300g of cream cheese. If necessary, heat in a 500W microwave for 10 to 20 seconds at a time, stirring with a rubber spatula each time. Add matcha and granulated sugar and mix well.
  3. Add 100g of mascarpone and mix with a rubber spatula.
  4. Crack 180g of whole eggs into a bowl and mix gently, being careful not to foam. If the eggs are cold, heat them in a warm, but not boiling, water bath.
  5. Add half of the eggs to the cheese dough and mix with a rubber spatula at the bottom of the bowl, making sure not to introduce any air.
  6. Add the remaining eggs and mix gently, using a rubber spatula and brushing around the bowl.
  7. Add part of the dough to 12g of rice flour and mix thoroughly, then add it to the entire dough and mix.
  8. Add 200g of fresh cream and mix gently with a rubber spatula. If you have just taken it out of the refrigerator, warm it up in a 500W microwave for 10 to 20 seconds before adding it.
  9. After completing the microwave process, preheat the oven to 250 degrees.
  10. Strain the batter through a strainer and pour into the prepared pan.
  11. Gently tap the mold against the counter to remove air so as not to spill it. If there are any small air bubbles on the surface, crush them with a bamboo skewer.
  12. Bake in an oven preheated to 250 degrees for 23-26 minutes. You can easily adjust the color by flipping the baking sheet about 13 minutes into the baking process.
  13. Once baked, let it cool on a baking sheet. Once it has cooled down, put it in the refrigerator and let it cool overnight.
  14. Warm a knife in hot water of about 50 degrees, wipe off the moisture, and then cut into pieces. Wipe the dough off the knife each time you cut it, then warm it up in the same way and wipe off any moisture.

Notes

  • The recommended temperature for cream cheese is 23-26 degrees, and for mascarpone and fresh cream it is 15-18 degrees.
  • If you heat the cream cheese all at once, it will separate and affect the texture and browning, so heat it in short bursts.
  • Gently mix the eggs to avoid incorporating air.
  • If you use rice flour with fine particles, such as for confectionery, it will have less of an effect on the texture.
  • If you don't cool it after baking, the center will be mushy. If it doesn't harden even after chilling overnight, it may be undercooked, and baking it 2 to 6 minutes longer next time may help.
  • If baking in a 12cm round mold, bake in an oven preheated to 240 degrees for about 22 minutes.
