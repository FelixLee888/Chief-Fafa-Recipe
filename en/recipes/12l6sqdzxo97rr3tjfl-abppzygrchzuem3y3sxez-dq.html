Ingredients

  • Cream cheese 300g for a 15cm removable mold
  • Matcha for mold 23g
  • 100g sugar
  • Mascarpone 100g for a 15cm mold with a removable bottom
  • 12g rice flour for mold
  • 200g fresh cream for a 15cm removable mold
  • Cream cheese 170g for 12cm round shape
  • Matcha 13g for cm round shape
  • Sugar 57g
  • Mascarpone 57g for 12cm round shape
  • cm round whole egg 102g
  • 7g rice flour for cm round shape
  • 114g fresh cream for 12cm round shape
  • Matcha 23g
  • 180g whole egg
  • 3 eggs
  • 12g rice flour
  • Matcha 13g
  • Whole egg 102g
  • 7g rice flour

Instructions

  1. Heat in the microwave at 500W for 10 to 20 seconds, stirring with a rubber spatula each time to prevent separation.
  2. Add 100g of mascarpone and keep at around 15-18 degrees.
  3. If you take it out of the refrigerator, warm it up at 500W for 10 to 20 seconds.
  4. Add half of Step 4 to the batter from Step 3 and mix with a rubber spatula.
  5. Add the remaining whole eggs and mix thoroughly, using a rubber spatula attached to the bottom.
  6. Measure out 12g of rice flour, add some of the batter and mix thoroughly to combine.
  7. Add 200g of fresh cream and mix gently with a rubber spatula.
  8. If you have just taken it out of the refrigerator, warm it up at 500W for 10 to 20 seconds.
  9. When the microwave is no longer in use, start preheating the oven to 250 degrees.
  10. If there are any small air bubbles on the surface, stir them with a bamboo skewer to eliminate them.
  11. Bake in an oven preheated to 250 degrees for 23 to 26 minutes.
  12. For better browning, open the oven once halfway through baking, rotate the baking sheet, and bake again.
  13. If necessary, baking for an additional 2 to 6 minutes may improve the results next time.
  14. Cheesecake that has been chilled overnight can be cut into sections neatly using a warm knife.
  15. Clean the knife in the same way when cutting again.
