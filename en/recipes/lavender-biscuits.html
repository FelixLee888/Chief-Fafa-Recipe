Lavender Biscuits

These lavender biscuits use fresh or dried lavender and are rolled in demerara sugar before baking. They should be just pale golden at the edges.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 15 min
Total: 1 hr
Servings: 40

Ingredients

  • 175g unsalted butter, softened
  • tbsp fresh lavender flowers and leaves, finely chopped (or 1tbsp
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 225g plain flour
  • 25g demerara sugar, for rolling

Instructions

  1. Cream the softened butter with the chopped fresh lavender, or dried lavender.
  2. Beat in the caster sugar.
  3. Stir in the flour to make a soft dough.
  4. Roll the dough into two logs and coat them in the demerara sugar.
  5. Chill the logs for 30 minutes.
  6. Slice into 20 biscuits.
  7. Bake at 160°C fan (180°C/325°F/Gas 3) for around 15 minutes, until just pale golden at the edges.

Notes

  • Use 2 tablespoons of fresh lavender flowers and leaves or 1 tablespoon of dried lavender.
  • The recipe description says it makes 40, while the method specifies slicing the dough into 20 biscuits.
