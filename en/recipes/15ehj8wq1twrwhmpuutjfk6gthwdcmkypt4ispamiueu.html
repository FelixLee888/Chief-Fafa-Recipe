Rich marron paste

A smooth and rich marron paste made from commercially available peeled sweet chestnuts. It can be used to spread on bread, make Mont Blanc and sweets.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Sweet chestnuts 300g
  • 70g sugar
  • 230g milk

Instructions

  1. Add the peeled sweet chestnuts, milk, and cane sugar to a pot.
  2. Heat over medium heat, stirring with a spatula.
  3. Once it boils, turn off the heat.
  4. Remove rough fever.
  5. Blend with a blender until smooth.
  6. Strain it to make it smooth to the touch.

Notes

  • If it is hard, add about 10-20g of milk to adjust it.
  • Since commercially available peeled sweet chestnuts are used, there is no need for preparation such as boiling or peeling raw chestnuts.
  • It can be used to spread on bread, make Mont Blanc and sweets.
