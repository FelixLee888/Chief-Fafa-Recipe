Rich black sesame terrine

A rich and filling terrine made with black sesame seeds and Greek yogurt. Just mix the ingredients and let it cool.

Prep: TBD
Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 100g unadjusted soy milk
  • 30g sugar
  • 15g black sesame
  • gelatin 5g
  • Greek yogurt 200g

Instructions

  1. Mix unadjusted soy milk, sugar beet, black sesame, and gelatin well and heat in a 600W microwave for about 1 minute and 20 seconds.
  2. Mix well until gelatin is dissolved.
  3. Add the Greek yogurt and mix well from the bottom until evenly mixed.
  4. Line a container of your choice with parchment paper or plastic wrap, and pour the mixed batter into it.
  5. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours to solidify.

Notes

  • Add the Greek yogurt and mix well from the bottom to make sure it's even.
