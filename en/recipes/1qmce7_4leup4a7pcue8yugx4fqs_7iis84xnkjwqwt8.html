Ingredients

  • Chestnuts 200g
  • 60g sugar
  • 90g milk
  • Maron Cream 150g from the finished product
  • Fresh cream 100cc
  • Milk 150cc
  • 20g sugar
  • 2-3g gelatin (soak the soaked type with 1 tablespoon of water)
  • 3 chestnuts for garnish

Instructions

  1. Put all the ingredients except marron cream in a pot, warm it up, add gelatin and mix well to dissolve.
  2. It will be easier to dissolve if you add the gelatin first.
  3. Add 150g of the marron cream from ① to the pot of ② and mix (the rest of the marron cream is for decoration).
  4. 好きな型に入れて固める。
