Rich marron pudding

Three 150ml containers of marron pudding made with rich marron cream made from peeled chestnuts. Use the remaining chestnut cream and chestnuts for garnish.

Cook: TBD

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Chestnuts 200g
  • 60g sugar
  • 90g milk
  • Maron Cream 150g from the finished product
  • Fresh cream 100cc
  • Milk 150cc
  • 20g sugar
  • 2-3g gelatin (soak the soaked type with 1 tablespoon of water)
  • 3 chestnuts

Instructions

  1. Separate 150g from the marron cream and keep the rest in the refrigerator.
  2. Add 150g of marron cream to the pot and mix.
  3. Pour into your favorite mold and let it harden.
  4. Once set, decorate with the remaining marron cream and chestnuts for decoration.

Notes

  • Adding the gelatin first will make it easier to dissolve.
  • If you use 2g of gelatin, it will be softer.
  • The rest of the marron cream will be used for decoration.
