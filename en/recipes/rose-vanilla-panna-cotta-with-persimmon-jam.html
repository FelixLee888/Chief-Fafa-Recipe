Ingredients

  • 150 ml heavy cream
  • 75 ml whole milk
  • 20 g sugar
  • ½ vanilla pod (or ½ tsp vanilla paste)
  • ¼ tsp rosewater
  • 1 ripe persimmon
  • 1–2 tsp agave syrup
  • 1–2 tsp lime juice
  • Zest of ½ lime
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 15 g toasted, crushed pistachios
  • Thin slices of fresh persimmon
  • Vanilla powder, for dusting

Instructions

  1. Gently heat the cream, milk, sugar and vanilla until steaming.
  2. Remove from the heat, stir in the rosewater and dissolve the gelatin.
  3. Pour into lightly oiled moulds and refrigerate for at least 5 hours, preferably overnight.
  4. Simmer it with the agave syrup, lime juice, lime zest and water for 10–15 minutes until soft.
  5. Blend until smooth and cool.
