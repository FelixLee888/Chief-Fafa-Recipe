Zephyr Mushrooms with Chocolate Glaze

Edible mushroom-shaped Zephyr treats made from piped stems and caps, finished with crushed Oreo “soil” or poppy seeds. An optional milk-and-chocolate agar glaze adds a shiny coating.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Zephyr mixture, prepared according to the previously shared Zephyr recipe
  • Crushed Oreo cookies, for the soil
  • Poppy seeds, as an alternative to Oreo soil
  • 100 ml milk
  • 15 g glucose syrup
  • 5 g agar-agar
  • 50 g chocolate

Instructions

  1. Pipe the Zephyr stems with pointed tops.
  2. Pipe the mushroom caps, then assemble the stems and caps quickly while the caps are still soft enough to insert the stems.
  3. Add crushed Oreo soil around the bottom of each stem while the Zephyr is still slightly sticky. Poppy seeds can be used instead.
  4. Let the mushrooms mature like regular Zephyr.
  5. For the glaze, combine the milk, agar-agar, and glucose syrup in a saucepan.
  6. Heat the mixture, stirring occasionally, following the source instruction to bring it to a boil without allowing it to boil.
  7. Melt the chocolate in the microwave in 20–25-second intervals, stirring between each interval.
  8. Pour the hot milk mixture into the melted chocolate and stir until smooth.
  9. Use the glaze immediately.

Notes

  • One batch makes approximately 24 mushrooms, depending on the size piped.
  • Zephyr sets quickly, so pipe the stems first and work fast during assembly.
  • The glaze sets very quickly and is not sticky once set.
  • If the glaze starts to set before use, gently reheat it and add a little milk if needed to restore its consistency.
