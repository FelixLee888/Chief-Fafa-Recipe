Instructions

  1. Cook the glaze bark: Add purified water, white sugar and dried osmanthus to the pot, stir until boiling.
  2. Add white jelly, stir evenly, then turn down to low heat and simmer for about 15 seconds.
  3. Pour into a patterned tray and refrigerate until completely set.
  4. Making yam puree: Peel and cut yam into pieces, steam until soft and mashed, about 20-30 minutes.
  5. Melt butter in another pot, pour in yam paste, add sugar and condensed milk, stir-fry over low heat until it forms a dough.
  6. Remove from heat and add peach fruit powder, stir evenly, divide half of the mixture and add dragon fruit powder to make a two-color yam puree.
  7. Combination and shaping: Knead the two-color yam puree into long strips, mix and roll into thin strips, and cut into small cylinders for filling.
  8. Cut the refrigerated glass skin into long strips, place the yam cylinder on it, and roll it up gently.
