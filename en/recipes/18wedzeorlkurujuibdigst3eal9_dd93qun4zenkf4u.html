Yam Glaze Roll

The crystal-clear and elastic glazed skin is made of sweet-scented osmanthus white jelly, wrapped in two-color yam puree with peach and dragon fruit flavors. When finished, it can be served as an afternoon tea snack or a specialty dessert in the restaurant.

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 45 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Purified water 300ml
  • White jelly 50g
  • Dried osmanthus appropriate amount
  • Yam 200g
  • Butter 15g
  • Condensed milk 10g
  • Peach fruit powder 25g
  • Dragon fruit powder appropriate amount
  • Chocolate flowers appropriate amount
  • Horse hoof popping beads appropriate amount
  • Pansy appropriate amount

Instructions

  1. Make glazed bark: Add purified water, 10g of white sugar and dried osmanthus to the pot, stir evenly and bring to a boil.
  2. Add white jelly, stir evenly, then turn down to low heat and simmer for 15 seconds.
  3. Pour the mixture into a patterned tray and refrigerate until completely set.
  4. Make yam puree: Peel and cut the yam into cubes, steam in a steamer for about 20 to 30 minutes, until soft.
  5. While hot, put the yam into a food processor and blend into a fine paste.
  6. Melt butter in another pot, add yam paste, 10g white sugar and condensed milk, stir-fry over low heat until it forms a dough.
  7. After turning off the heat, add peach fruit powder and stir evenly; divide the yam puree into two halves, add dragon fruit powder to one half and mix it into two colors.
  8. Knead the two colors of yam paste into long strips, mix and roll into thin strips, and then cut into small cylinders for filling.
  9. Take out the solidified glass skin, cut it into long strips, put it on the yam cylinder and roll it up gently.
  10. Place the yam glazed rolls on a glazed plate and garnish with chocolate flowers, popping horseshoe beads and pansies.

Notes

  • The glazed leather belt has the fragrance of osmanthus, and the yam filling combines the fruity aroma of peach and dragon fruit.
  • It is recommended to finish eating on the same day if the normal temperature is below 25℃.
  • It can be stored for 2 to 3 days when refrigerated at 0 to 4°C. Please confirm that it has not deteriorated before consumption.
