Ingredients

  • 🔸(12-15 pieces)
  • Glutinous rice flour 160g
  • 1 egg
  • Fresh milk 200ml
  • Unsalted butter 30g
  • sugar 50g
  • salt 1g
  • How to do it🔹
  • Heat the fresh milk to 40 degrees, put the eggs at room temperature; dissolve the butter in hot water
  • Mix all ingredients and sift
  • Grease the mold with softened butter, not too thin; pour the batter until it is 80% full
  • Bake at 190 degrees for 15 minutes, then bake at 180 degrees for 15-20 minutes; unmold and enjoy
