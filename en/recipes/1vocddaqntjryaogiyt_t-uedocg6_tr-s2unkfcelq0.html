Crispy Butter Rice Cake

This butter rice cake is crispy on the outside, soft and smoky on the inside, and has a rich butter aroma. Just mix the ingredients and bake them. You can make about 12 to 15 pieces.

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 35 min
Total: 45 min
Servings: 約可製作12至15個

Ingredients

  • Glutinous rice flour 160g
  • 1 egg
  • Fresh milk 200ml
  • Unsalted butter 30g
  • sugar 50g
  • salt 1g

Instructions

  1. Heat the milk to 40°C and bring the eggs to room temperature.
  2. Melt unsalted butter in a pot of immersed water.
  3. Mix all ingredients well and sift.
  4. Coat the mold with softened butter. Do not spread the butter too thinly; pour the batter until it is 80% full.
  5. Bake at 190°C for 15 minutes, then adjust to 180°C for 15 to 20 minutes.
  6. After taking it out of the oven, unmold and enjoy.

Notes

  • Makes approximately 12 to 15 servings.
  • The finished product is crispy on the outside and soft, waxy and smoky on the inside.
