Glazed Roast Duck with Orange Sauce

A whole duck is roasted in portions with a glossy spiced orange glaze, then served with a lightly thickened orange and Grand Marnier sauce. The duck is rested before carving and finished with fresh orange segments.

Prep: 25 min
Cook: 1 hr 45 min
Total: 10 hr 10 min
Servings: with a glossy spiced orange glaze, then serv

Ingredients

  • whole duck, approximately 2.2kg
  • Salt, for seasoning
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 star anise
  • 4 cm ginger, sliced
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey, for the glaze
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 200 ml orange juice, for the glaze
  • 30 g butter, plus extra to finish the sauce
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • 150 ml Grand Marnier
  • 500 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp honey, for the sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 150 ml orange juice, for the sauce
  • 1 tsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp water
  • Salt and pepper, to season the sauce
  • 1–2 oranges, segmented, for finishing

Instructions

  1. Season the duck all over with salt and leave it uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Roast the legs and wing tips at 150°C for 1 hour, until tender.
  3. Meanwhile, make the glaze. Combine the cinnamon, star anise, ginger, brown sugar, 2 tbsp honey, orange zest, white wine vinegar, soy sauce and 200 ml orange juice in a pan.
  4. Bring the glaze to a boil and reduce until thick, glossy and able to coat a spoon.
  5. Remove the legs from the oven and increase the oven temperature to 210°C.
  6. Place the legs, wing tips and crown in a low casserole pan. Brush with glaze and roast for 10 minutes. Glaze again and roast for another 10 minutes, then glaze once more and roast for a final 10 minutes.
  7. Monitor the breast and remove it when the internal temperature reaches 50°C; it should rise to approximately 55°C while resting. Rest the duck uncovered in a warm place.
  8. While the duck rests, melt 30 g butter in a pan and soften the rosemary and thyme.
  9. Add the Grand Marnier and flambé. Add the chicken stock, 1 tbsp honey, Dijon mustard and 150 ml orange juice, then simmer for 15 minutes.
  10. Strain the sauce, finish it with extra butter and season with salt and pepper.
  11. Mix the cornflour with the water to make a slurry. Add it to the sauce, adding more if needed, until the sauce is glossy and lightly thickened rather than gloopy.
  12. Stir in the segmented oranges.
  13. Carve the rested duck, spoon over the orange sauce and finish with extra orange segments.

Notes

  • The breast should be removed at 50°C and will continue rising to approximately 55°C as it rests.
  • Rest the duck uncovered in a warm place, such as a warming oven.
  • Add more cornflour slurry only as needed; the sauce should be lightly thickened and glossy, not gloopy.
