Instructions

  1. Add the beef stock, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard and bay leaves.
  2. Simmer for 5 minutes until slightly thickened, then season to taste.
  3. Mix the softened butter with the grated garlic, thyme leaves, salt and pepper.
  4. Carefully spread the mixture under the chicken skin, then rub the outside with 2 tbsp olive oil and season generously with salt and pepper.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, then finish with fresh thyme and black pepper.
  6. Carve and serve with buttery mashed potatoes and the cheesy onion gravy.
