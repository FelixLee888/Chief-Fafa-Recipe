Ingredients

  • 200g fresh cream
  • Earl Gray 10g
  • Cream cheese 350g
  • 100g sugar
  • Sour cream 90g
  • 12g rice flour
  • 3 whole eggs

Instructions

  1. Combine 200g of fresh cream and tea leaves in a handheld pot and heat while stirring over low heat (I used T2 Earl Gray tea leaves).
  2. When using the microwave, heat at 500W for 10 to 20 seconds at a time, stirring with a rubber spatula each time.
  3. Add 100g of granulated sugar and knead until smooth.
  4. Add 90g of sour cream and mix with a rubber spatula.
  5. If you have just taken it out of the refrigerator, warm it up at 500W for 10 to 20 seconds.
  6. Crack 3 whole eggs and 1 egg yolk into a bowl and mix without whisking.
  7. Raise the temperature by boiling water.
  8. Mix gently, making sure not to introduce any air into the eggs.
  9. Add half of the batter from step 3 to step 4 and mix with a rubber spatula.
  10. After adding the eggs, air tends to enter the batter, so mix with a rubber spatula attached to the bottom of the bowl.
  11. Add the remaining whole eggs and mix gently with a rubber spatula.
  12. Mix while brushing the surrounding area.
  13. Add the boiled black tea and mix well.
  14. Weigh out 12g of rice flour, add some of the batter and mix thoroughly until combined.
  15. Start preheating the oven to 250 degrees.
  16. Bake in the oven at 250 degrees for 25-27 minutes.
  17. Open the oven once halfway through the baking process, flip the baking sheet over, and bake again to ensure even browning.
  18. If the inside is mushy, bake it for an additional 2 to 6 minutes next time.
  19. Cut the cheesecake that has cooled overnight using a warm knife.
  20. Warm the knife in hot water of about 50 degrees, wipe the blade, and cut.
