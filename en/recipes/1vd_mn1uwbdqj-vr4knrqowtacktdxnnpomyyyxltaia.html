corn mousse

A mousse made by combining corn puree with gelatin and fresh cream. Makes 6 100ml cups.

Prep: TBD
Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Approximately 4 corn
  • 1 onion
  • Corn puree 350g
  • gelatin 9g
  • Fresh cream 90ml

Instructions

  1. Make a puree with the corn and onions.
  2. Use 350g of the corn puree you made for the mousse.
  3. Combine corn puree with gelatin and fresh cream.

Notes

  • This recipe uses simple ingredients to make it easy to make at home.
