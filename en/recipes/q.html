It’s more delicious. Butter-roasted rice cakes. 12 servings. One egg at room temperature. 40g sugar. 25g condensed milk. 5g honey. 1g salt. 132g glutinous rice flour. 30g corn flour. 200g warm milk. 40g melted butter. Mix everything together and beat until there are no powder lumps. The mold should be coated with thick cream. Pour into the mold, about 8 minutes full. Preheat the oven and bake at 180 degrees for 38 minutes.

Ingredients

  • One egg at room temperature
  • Sugar 40g
  • 煉乳 25g
  • Honey 5g
  • salt 1g
  • 糯米粉 132g
  • Corn flour 30g
  • Warm milk 200g
  • 融化奶油 40g
  • 再氣炸鍋180度烤5分鐘 更好吃喔 黃油烤年糕 12顆

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together until there are no lumps.
  2. 倒入模具，約8分滿，分成12顆。
  3. Preheat the oven to 180 degrees and bake for 38 minutes.
  4. If using an air fryer, bake at 180 degrees for 5 minutes.
