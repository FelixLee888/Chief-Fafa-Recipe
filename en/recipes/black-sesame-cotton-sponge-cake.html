Black Sesame Cotton Sponge Cake

A cotton-style sponge cake made with ground black sesame, milk, and separated eggs. The batter is lightened with whipped egg whites and baked using a water bath.

Prep: are the water bath for baking
Cook: 10 min
Total: 25 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 70 ml corn oil
  • 65 g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp corn starch
  • 50 g ground black sesame
  • 1 whole egg
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 70 ml milk
  • 5 egg whites
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 95 g caster sugar

Instructions

  1. Warm the corn oil.
  2. Add the ground black sesame, egg yolks, and milk to the batter mixture.
  3. Whisk the egg whites until stiff peaks form.
  4. Mix one-third of the whipped egg white mixture into the batter.
  5. Pour the batter into a baking tin.
  6. Preheat the oven for 10 minutes.
  7. Pour water into a large tray to prepare the water bath for baking.

Notes

  • The cake is baked using a water bath.
  • No oven temperature or baking time was provided.
