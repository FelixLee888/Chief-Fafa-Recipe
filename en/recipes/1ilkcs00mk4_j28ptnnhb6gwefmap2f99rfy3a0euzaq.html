Sweet chestnut mousse

A rich and smooth marron mousse made using peeled sweet chestnuts. Mix in the fresh cream and let it cool and harden in the refrigerator.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Sweet chestnuts 100g
  • 50ml milk
  • 10g beet sugar
  • Fresh cream 100ml
  • 2.5g powdered gelatin
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Place peeled sweet chestnuts, milk, and beet sugar in a heat-resistant container and heat in a 600W microwave for 1 minute.
  2. Blend with a blender or mixer until smooth.
  3. Add powdered gelatin to water, soften, and heat in a 600W microwave for 10 seconds.
  4. Add gelatin and salt to the marron paste, mix, and let cool to about human body temperature.
  5. Whisk the fresh cream for 7 minutes until it forms soft peaks.
  6. Add 1/3 of the fresh cream to the marron paste and mix well.
  7. Add the remaining fresh cream in two parts and mix gently with a rubber spatula.
  8. Pour into a mold and chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours to harden.

Notes

  • Beet sugar can be replaced with cane sugar or regular sugar.
