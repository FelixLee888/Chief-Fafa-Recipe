Ingredients

  • Sweet chestnut... 100g
  • Milk...50ml
  • Beet sugar... 10g
  • Fresh cream...100ml
  • Powdered gelatin...2.5g
  • Water...1 tablespoon
  • A pinch of salt...

Instructions

  1. Put peeled sweet chestnuts, milk, and beet sugar in a heat-resistant container and heat at 600W for 1 minute.
  2. Blend with a blender or mixer until smooth.
  3. Add gelatin to water, soften, and heat at 600W for 10 seconds.
  4. Add gelatin and salt to the marron paste, mix, and cool to human body temperature.
  5. Whisk the fresh cream until it forms soft peaks (beat for 7 minutes).
  6. Add 1/3 of the fresh cream to the marron paste and mix well.
  7. Add the remaining fresh cream in two parts and mix gently with a rubber spatula.
  8. Pour into a mold and chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours to complete.
