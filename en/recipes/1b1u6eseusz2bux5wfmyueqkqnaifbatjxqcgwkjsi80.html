Red Oolong Canelé

This is a recipe that can make about 12 pieces of red oolong tea-flavored kariru, with a thin and crispy shell and a red oolong flavor. The batter needs to be refrigerated for 12 to 24 hours. After baking, cool to room temperature and then freeze.

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 55 min
Total: 20 hr 45 min
Servings: 12顆

Ingredients

  • High gluten flour 85g
  • Red Oolong Tea Powder 15g
  • Milk 500g
  • Cream 50g
  • 2 whole eggs
  • salt 1g
  • Fine sugar 220g
  • rum 30g

Instructions

  1. Heat the milk, cream, red oolong tea powder and salt in a small pot and cook until the cream melts and small bubbles appear around the pot. Turn off the heat, cover the pot and simmer for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix fine sugar and whole eggs evenly, add sifted high-gluten flour and mix evenly.
  3. After the milk liquid has cooled to about 40°C, pour it into the batter, stir evenly, and then add rum.
  4. Cover the batter with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 12 to 24 hours.
  5. Before baking, preheat the oven to 250°C (top and bottom), take out the batter and let it cool down at room temperature for 1 hour.
  6. Coat the crepe mold evenly and thinly with melted butter and refrigerate until the butter solidifies.
  7. Stir the batter well and sift into a measuring cup before baking. Pour 75g of batter into each mold, stirring once before each pour.
  8. First bake at 250°C for 15 minutes with upper and lower heat, then adjust to 170°C with upper heat and 250°C with lower heat for 40 minutes. The actual temperature and time are adjusted according to the characteristics of the oven.
  9. After taking it out of the oven, let it come to room temperature, and then put it in the freezer for storage.

Notes

  • The original recipe produces a thinner and crispier crust; those who prefer a thicker crust can extend the baking time.
  • The original recipe uses Portuguese Grilo aluminum clearo molds.
  • The original recipe uses a Yamasaki oven.
