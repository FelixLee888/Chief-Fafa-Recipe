Prep: 15 min
Cook: 30 min
Total: 45 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • High gluten flour 85g
  • Red Oolong Tea Powder 15g
  • Milk 500g
  • Cream 50g
  • 2 whole eggs
  • salt 1g
  • Fine sugar 220g
  • rum 30g

Instructions

  1. Put the milk, cream, red oolong tea powder and salt into a small pot and cook until the butter melts and small bubbles appear around the pot. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix fine sugar and whole eggs evenly, add sifted high-gluten flour and mix evenly.
  3. Pour the milk that has cooled to about 40 degrees into the batter, stir evenly and add rum.
  4. Preheat the oven to 250 degrees;
  5. Mix batter well and sift into measuring cup;
  6. Pour 75g of batter into each mold and stir once before and after pouring.
  7. After taking it out of the oven, cool it to room temperature and then store it in the freezer.
