Fermented Blueberry Soda

A naturally carbonated blueberry soda made by fermenting blueberries, ginger, optional cardamom or cinnamon, brown sugar, and water. The drink undergoes a first fermentation in a jar, followed by a second fermentation in a flip-top bottle.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 22 hr
Total: 22 hr 15 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 3 cardamom pods or cinnamon, optional
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 25 L water

Instructions

  1. Combine the blueberries, ginger, optional cardamom or cinnamon, brown sugar, and water in a jar.
  2. Set the jar aside for 4 days for the first fermentation.
  3. On days 2 and 3, mix well and return the jar to rest.
  4. On day 4, strain the mixture.
  5. Transfer the strained liquid to a flip-top bottle for the second fermentation.
  6. Leave the bottle for up to 2 days, or stop after 20–24 hours if you want less fizz and to reduce the risk of overflow.
  7. Refrigerate the bottle after the second fermentation.

Notes

  • The sugar is consumed by naturally occurring yeast on the berries, creating carbonation.
  • The length of the second fermentation affects how fizzy the soda becomes.
  • Refrigerate after the second fermentation.
