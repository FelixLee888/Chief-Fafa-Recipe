Fluffy azuki mousse

A light Japanese-style mousse that melts in your mouth, made by combining boiled azuki beans and fresh cream and gently solidifying it with gelatin. Add whipped cream and boiled azuki beans to finish.

Ingredients

  • [Azuki mousse] Fresh cream (40%) 100g
  • 10g sugar
  • [Azuki mousse] Boiled azuki beans 100g
  • [Azuki mousse] 3 tablespoons water
  • [Azuki mousse] Fresh cream (40%) 30g
  • [Azuki mousse] 2g powdered gelatin
  • 10g cold water
  • [Finishing] Fresh cream (40%) 60g
  • 6g sugar
  • [Finish] Appropriate amount of boiled azuki beans

Instructions

  1. Combine boiled azuki beans and 100g of fresh cream.
  2. Add plenty of whipped cream and boiled azuki beans to the hardened mousse.

Notes

  • A refreshing Japanese-style sweet with a moderate sweetness.
