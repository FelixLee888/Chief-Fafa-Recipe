honey butter rice flour bread

This rice flour bread is made with rice flour for bread making and has the flavor of honey and butter. This is the amount for one loaf of bread.

Ingredients

  • Rice flour for bread making “Mizuhochikara” 290g
  • honey 50g
  • 7g salt
  • 5g dry yeast
  • 210ml of lukewarm water (40℃)
  • 20g melted butter

Instructions

  1. Line a bread mold with lined paper and wrap the lid with aluminum foil for frying. Melt the butter in a 600W microwave for 20 to 30 seconds.
  2. Combine rice flour, honey, salt, dry yeast, lukewarm water, and melted butter in a bowl and mix together. If the dough is stiff, add a little warm water while keeping an eye on it.
  3. Once the batter is fully incorporated, pour it into a bread mold and cover with plastic wrap.
  4. Let the dough rise until it becomes about 1.8 to 2 times the height. If fermenting at room temperature, leave it as is. Preheat the oven to 180℃. If you are using the oven's fermentation function, let it rise for 25-30 minutes at 40℃, then take it out early and let it stand at room temperature, while preheating the oven to 180℃.
  5. Remove the plastic wrap, cover, and bake at 180℃ for 40 minutes.
  6. Remove the lid and bake at 210℃ for about 18 minutes to brown the surface.
  7. Once baked, remove from the mold and remove from heat.

Notes

  • If you let it rise too long, the center of the dough may dent after the first baking.
  • Store baked bread in a ziplock bag.
