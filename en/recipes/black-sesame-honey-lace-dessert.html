Black Sesame Honey Lace Dessert

🖤 BLACK SESAME HONEY LACE DESSERT | Crispy, Creamy & Delicate!

Prep: TBD
Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Black Sesame Seeds (finely ground) – 25 g
  • All-Purpose Flour – 20 g
  • Powdered Sugar – 25 g
  • Egg White – 30 g
  • Melted Butter – 20 g
  • Milk – 15 ml

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients into a smooth, thin batter. Spread a thin layer in a non-stick pan over low-medium heat. Cook until a natural lace pattern forms and moisture evaporates. Carefully lift while warm and shape over a curved mould. Cool until crisp.
  2. 🍯 HONEY GEL
  3. Ingredients:
  4. Honey – 50 g
  5. Water – 30 ml
  6. Lemon Juice – 5 ml
  7. Agar-Agar – 1 g
  8. Boil water and agar-agar until dissolved. Add honey and lemon juice. Pour into a dish and chill until set. Blend until smooth.
  9. 🤍 WHITE CHOCOLATE CREAM
  10. Ingredients:
  11. White Chocolate – 100 g
  12. Mascarpone – 100 g
  13. Vanilla Extract – ½ tsp
  14. Melt chocolate with 70 ml warm cream and cool. Whip mascarpone, remaining 130 ml cold cream and vanilla. Fold in cooled chocolate and whip until fluffy.
  15. ✨ ASSEMBLY
  16. Pipe white chocolate cream into cooled lace shells. Add honey gel in the centre and garnish with black sesame seeds and edible flowers. Serve immediately for the best crunch!
