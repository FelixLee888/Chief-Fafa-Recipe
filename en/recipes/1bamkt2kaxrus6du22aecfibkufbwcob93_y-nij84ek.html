Ingredients

  • 2 persimmons
  • 50g butter
  • 40g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 70ml soy milk
  • Rice flour 80g

Instructions

  1. Put the butter in a heat-resistant container and heat it at 600W for 20 seconds to melt it.
  2. Break the eggs into a bowl, add the sugar and mix.
  3. Add soy milk, rice flour, and melted butter in that order and mix.
  4. Add thinly sliced ​​persimmons and mix everything together.
  5. Pour the dough into a pound mold and bake in an oven heated to 180℃ for about 45 minutes.
  6. ⭐︎Put the butter in a heat-resistant container and heat at 600W for 20 seconds.
  7. ① Break the eggs into a bowl, add sugar and mix.
  8. ② Add soy milk, rice flour, and melted butter in that order and mix.
  9. ④Put in a pound mold and place in an oven heated to 180℃.
