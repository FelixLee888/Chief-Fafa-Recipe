persimmon invisible

Invisible persimmon is made by mixing thinly sliced persimmons into the batter and baking them. This is for one 18cm pound mold.

Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 2 persimmons
  • 50g butter
  • 40g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 70ml soy milk
  • Rice flour 80g

Instructions

  1. Put the butter in a heat-resistant container and heat it in a 600W microwave for 20 seconds to melt it.
  2. Cut the persimmon into 8 pieces, remove the stem, peel the skin, and cut into thin slices.
  3. Set the liner in an 18cm pound mold.
  4. Break the eggs into a bowl, add the sugar and mix.
  5. Add soy milk, rice flour, and melted butter in that order, stirring each time.
  6. Add the thinly sliced persimmons and mix everything together.
  7. Pour the dough into a pound mold and bake in an oven heated to 180℃ for about 45 minutes.

Notes

  • If it starts to burn while baking, cover with aluminum foil.
