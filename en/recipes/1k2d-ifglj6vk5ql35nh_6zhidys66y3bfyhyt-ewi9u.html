Tea perfume jam mango cold dish

Tea Perfume Jam Mango Cold Dishes: The most anticipated thing in summer is the mango season! The sweetness of mango, the citrusy aroma of Earl Gray tea, lemon juice and olive oil are mixed to create a tea perfume jam that is sweet and sour. It is paired with the aroma of yogurt, frankincense, salty raw ham, and sesame leaves. Finally, it is embellished with the fragrance of lemon peel. It is a seasonal limited fruit cold dish. The Aiwen mango in Fangshan, Pingtung, smiles every day. @viviyaciou has cultivated it for nearly a month on this piece of land to convey this warmth.
