Ingredients

  • Carrots: 2
  • Olive oil: 2 teaspoons
  • Salt koji: 1 tablespoon
  • Grated cheese: as desired
  • Black pepper: as needed
  • Parsley: as needed
  • Yogurt: 1 tablespoon
  • Grain mustard: 1 teaspoon
  • Honey: 1 teaspoon

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200℃.
  2. Cut the carrots into 4 to 8 pieces depending on the size.
  3. Arrange on a baking sheet, sprinkle with black pepper, and bake in an oven at 200℃ for 20 to 30 minutes.
  4. Make the sauce by mixing yogurt, grain mustard, and honey.
  5. Add the sauce to the roasted carrots and sprinkle with parsley if desired.
  6. Cut 2 carrots into 4-8 pieces depending on the size.
  7. Arrange on a baking sheet, sprinkle with black pepper, and bake in an oven at 200℃ for 20 to 30 minutes.
  8. Mix yogurt, grain mustard, and honey to make a sauce and add it to the roasted carrots.
