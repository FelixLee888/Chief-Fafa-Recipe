Grilled sea cucumber with green onion

The sea cucumber is cooked with green onions and scallion oil fried until golden brown, giving it a rich aroma. To make it, you need to prepare the scallion oil, base soup and sea cucumber first, and then thicken it and serve it on the plate.

Prep: 20 min
Cook: 20 min
Total: 40 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • sea cucumber
  • green onions
  • Lard
  • cooking oil
  • ginger slices
  • coriander head
  • chicken soup
  • light soy sauce
  • Old soy sauce
  • star anise
  • white pepper
  • Cornstarch water

Instructions

  1. Cut green onions into sections and set aside sliced green onions.
  2. Add lard to the wok, fry the green onions until golden brown, remove and set aside.
  3. Add chicken broth, light soy sauce, sugar and wine to the original wok and steam for about 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. In a separate wok, add cooking oil, add ginger slices, scallion slices and coriander heads to make scallion oil.
  5. Boil the sea cucumber in water for about 2 minutes, remove and set aside.
  6. Return the remaining ginger and scallion from the fried scallion oil to the wok, add star anise, wine and light soy sauce and sauté until fragrant, then add chicken stock and cook to make a base soup.
  7. Add sugar to the wok, stir-fry the sugar color, add sea cucumber, scallion oil, wine, light soy sauce and base soup, and simmer over low heat for about 10 minutes.
  8. Season with white pepper and dark soy sauce, add the previous soup, and then cover it with cornstarch water.
  9. After serving, drizzle with scallion oil.

Notes

  • Sea cucumbers need to be boiled in water for about 2 minutes.
  • The scallions are first fried in lard until golden brown. You can use fried scallion oil to make scallion oil and add flavor.
