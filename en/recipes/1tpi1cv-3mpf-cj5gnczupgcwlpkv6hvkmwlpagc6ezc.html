Adult black sesame parfait

A parfait that combines water jelly, black sesame mascarpone cream, black sesame ice cream, and warabi mochi style to enjoy the texture and rich black sesame flavor. Makes 2 glasses.

Ingredients

  • 200ml water
  • Pearl agar 4g
  • case 4g
  • 1 tablespoon oligosaccharide syrup
  • Mascarpone 200g
  • Agave syrup 40g
  • 2-3 tablespoons black sesame paste (unsweetened)
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 200g fresh cream
  • 70g sugar
  • Tapioca flour (or potato starch) 50g
  • 40g sugar
  • 250ml water
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 120ml water
  • Black sugar syrup Appropriate amount of commercially available brown sugar syrup

Instructions

  1. Make water jelly. Add pearl agar little by little to water and oligosaccharide syrup and mix well. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and heat for 1 to 2 minutes.
  2. Pour the water jelly into a glass and chill in the refrigerator to solidify. If using gelatin, turn off the heat, then add the gelatin soaked in water and let it dissolve in the residual heat. Do not boil the gelatin solution.
  3. Make black sesame cream and ice cream. Whisk the fresh cream and castor sugar thoroughly.
  4. Mix black sesame paste and milk, add mascarpone and agave syrup, and make smooth.
  5. Add whipped cream and mix until fluffy, then separate into cream and ice cream. Put the cream into a piping bag and chill it in the freezer for 2 to 3 hours to solidify it for ice cream.
  6. Make warabi mochi style. Add tapioca flour or potato starch, cane sugar, and water to a pot and mix well with a whisk.
  7. Place over low heat and stir constantly with a wooden spoon. When it turns a glossy amber color and starts to stretch, turn off the heat.
  8. Fill a round ice cube tray with bracken mochi and cool in ice water to make it round.
  9. If you are using homemade black sugar syrup, put the brown sugar and water in a pot and boil over low heat until it thickens a little, then let it cool. You can also use commercially available brown sugar syrup.
  10. Serve. Layer water jelly, brown sugar syrup, black sesame cream, crumble, warabi mochi style, black sesame ice cream, and brown sugar syrup in that order.

Notes

  • The amount of water jelly is the revised content.
  • If using gelatin, turn off the heat and add it before boiling, as it will not harden.
  • Warabimochi-style can be made into a round shape by using a round ice cube tray.
  • The crumbles are listed in the order of presentation, but the details of ingredients and preparation are not listed in the supplied recipe.
