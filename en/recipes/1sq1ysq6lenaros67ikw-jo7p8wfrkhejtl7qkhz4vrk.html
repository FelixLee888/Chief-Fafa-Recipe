Crispy cheese mochi

Crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, this savory cheese mochi snack is made with tapioca flour and glutinous rice flour. The recipe contains no additional sugar, and the saltiness of the cheese brings out the flavor.

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 25 min
Total: 35 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Milk 180g
  • Olive oil 80g
  • 1 egg
  • Cassava flour 125g
  • Glutinous rice flour 35g
  • cheese 80g

Instructions

  1. Mix milk, olive oil, eggs, tapioca flour, glutinous rice flour and cheese, and stir into a smooth paste.
  2. Pour the mixture into the mold and level the surface.
  3. Bake in an oven preheated to 200°C for about 25 minutes, until the mochi puffs up and is colored.
  4. Adjust the actual baking time according to the size of the oven and mold, and enjoy hot after taking it out of the oven.

Notes

  • Shredded cheese, cheese slices, cheese cubes or cheese balls can be used, but the moisture, saltiness and melting effect of different cheeses will be different.
  • This snack has no added sugar and has a salty cheese flavor.
  • The skin is the crispiest when freshly baked, so it is recommended to eat it while it is hot.
