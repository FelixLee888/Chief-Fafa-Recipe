Matcha Madeleine

Refrigerate the matcha madeleine batter for at least 3 hours before baking. Brush it with milk while it is still hot to make it dry on the outside and moist on the inside. Can be filled with matcha white chocolate filling.

Prep: 25 min
Cook: 10 min
Total: 35 min
Servings: 約可製作淺模9顆或深模6顆

Ingredients

  • Low gluten flour 35g
  • Corn flour 5g
  • Baking powder 1.5g
  • Fine sugar 30g
  • Jelly 25g
  • pinch of salt
  • Fermented butter 50g
  • Milk (for brushing after taking out the oven)
  • White chocolate 25g
  • Fresh cream 50g
  • Matcha powder 5g

Instructions

  1. Microwave the fermented butter for about 20 seconds until melted and the temperature is about 40 degrees, set aside.
  2. Beat the egg liquid, heat over water to 30 degrees, or microwave for about 10 seconds; add fine sugar, starch syrup and salt, stir evenly.
  3. Sift the low-gluten flour, corn flour, baking powder and matcha powder into the egg mixture and mix evenly.
  4. Add melted cream, mix well, cover and store, refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  5. Brush the mold with remaining butter and refrigerate until ready.
  6. Make the filling: Heat the white chocolate over water until it melts, add the sifted matcha powder and mix well, then add the fresh cream and mix well, then refrigerate until ready.
  7. Place a baking pan in the oven and preheat the upper and lower heat to 190 degrees.
  8. Stir the refrigerated batter evenly and squeeze it into the mold about 8 minutes full.
  9. Bake at 190 degrees for 11 minutes with upper and lower heat, and adjust according to your oven conditions.
  10. After taking it out of the oven, brush it with two layers of milk while it's still hot, let it absorb, and then let it cool.
  11. If making filling, take out the refrigerated filling and beat with a whisk.
  12. Dig a hole in the middle of the madeleine at the navel and squeeze in the matcha white chocolate filling.

Notes

  • The quantity is enough to make 9 shallow molds or 6 deep molds.
  • The filling-free version has been tested at room temperature overnight, refrigerated for 2 days and frozen for several days, and the texture is not dry.
  • Filled madeleines need to be kept frozen.
  • Brushing the madeleines with milk will keep the outside dry to the touch and the inside moist after the madeleines cool.
  • The amount of matcha powder can be increased to 7-10g according to preference to achieve a stronger matcha flavor.
