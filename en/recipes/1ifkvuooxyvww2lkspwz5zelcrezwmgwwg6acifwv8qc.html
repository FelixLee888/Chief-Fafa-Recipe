Beef boiled in shrimp soup

Beef ribs are boiled with shrimp skin, carrots and butter to make a rich shrimp soup, which is then thickened and stewed in a pot. The beef is braised until the meat separates from the bones and is suitable for eating with rice or bread.

Prep: are 15g
Cook: 2 hr 50 min
Total: 3 hr 20 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Cowboy ribs
  • sea salt
  • carrot
  • Shopee 15g
  • Cracked black pepper 3g
  • butter
  • Hot water 750ml

Instructions

  1. Drain the beef ribs, sprinkle with sea salt, and marinate for 10 minutes.
  2. Cut carrots into cubes, prepare dried shrimps and cracked black pepper.
  3. Drain the water from the beef ribs again, fry over medium heat until the surface is caramelized, and transfer to the stew pot.
  4. Sauté carrots and dried shrimps in butter, about 3 minutes.
  5. Pour 750ml hot water into the frying pan and bring to a boil, then pour into the saucepan.
  6. Cook over low heat for 2.5 hours, turning the beef ribs over once halfway through.
  7. Remove the beef ribs and remove the bones, stir the soup and the ingredients in the pot until smooth and thick.
  8. Return the beef and puree to the stew pot and simmer over low heat for a further 15 minutes.
  9. Cut the beef into pieces and put on a plate, pour the soup over it.

Notes

  • After being stirred, the soup becomes thicker and has the salty flavor of dried shrimps.
  • The cowboy bones will be braised until the bones and meat separate and melt in your mouth.
  • Enjoy with rice or bread.
