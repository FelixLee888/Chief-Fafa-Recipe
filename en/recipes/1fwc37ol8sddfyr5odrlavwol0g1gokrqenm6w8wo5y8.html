Sake-poached abalone with abalone liver pate

The abalone is slow-cooked in a broth flavored with kombu, sake, soy sauce and mirin, and is served with a thick liver pate made from abalone liver.

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 1 hr 5 min
Total: 1 hr 15 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 5 abalone
  • Shaoxing wine a little
  • salt a little
  • Kombu 5g
  • soy sauce 2 tablespoons
  • sake 2 tablespoons
  • Mirin 2 tablespoons
  • sugar 2 tsp
  • Bonito soup dumplings 2 pieces
  • Water 700mL
  • 1 egg yolk
  • soy sauce 1 tablespoon
  • Light cream 1 tablespoon
  • sugar a little

Instructions

  1. Add a little Shaoxing wine and salt to boiling water, add abalone and sear for about 2 minutes. After taking it out, remove the shell, separate the abalone meat from the internal organs, and tear off the abalone mouth.
  2. Prepare soup with kombu, soy sauce, sake, mirin, sugar, bonito soup bag and water. Add abalone and cook over low heat for 1 hour.
  3. Add abalone liver to the soup and cook for about 5 minutes, take out and sieve.
  4. Mix the sifted abalone liver with egg yolk, soy sauce, light cream and a little sugar.
  5. Cook the liver paste over low heat until thickened and sieve again.

Notes

  • After the abalone liver pate is completed, it can also be used for stir-fry.
