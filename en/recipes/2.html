2-layer cheesecake with grilled cheese and rare cheese

It is made with two layers: a rich baked cheesecake wrapped in chewy gyuhi and a smooth rare cheesecake. Rare cheese is divided into three types: plain, matcha, and chocolate.

Ingredients

  • [Baked cheese] Cream cheese 125g
  • [Baked cheese] Sour cream 45g
  • 50g sugar
  • [Baked cheese] Cornstarch 4g
  • 1g salt
  • 50g whole eggs
  • [Baked cheese] Fresh cream 35% 55g
  • [Rare cheese] Cream cheese 65g
  • 10g sugar
  • [Rare cheese] Powdered gelatin 1g
  • 5g water
  • [Rare cheese] Fresh cream 35% 80g
  • [Finishing] Gyuhi 5 pieces
  • [Finishing] Powdered sugar for topping (optional)
  • [Finishing] Matcha powder (optional)
  • [Finishing] Cocoa powder (optional)

Instructions

  1. Make the baked cheese dough and bake at 130℃ for 14-15 minutes.
  2. Make rare cheese dough and divide it into 3 types: plain, matcha, and chocolate.
  3. Place the rare cheese batter on top of the baked cheesecake.
  4. Chill and harden in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours.
  5. Wrap it in Gyuhi crepe and shape into a cube.
  6. Sprinkle powdered sugar, matcha powder, and cocoa powder to taste.

Notes

  • Rare cheese comes in three types: plain, matcha, and chocolate.
  • Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours to solidify, then wrap in Gyuhi.
  • Matcha rare cheese is for 3 pieces, chocolate rare cheese is for 3 pieces.
