salted lemon

Mix the juiced green lemons with sea salt, sugar, lemon juice and spices. Press tightly into a clean container and refrigerate for 3 to 4 weeks. The finished product is sour, salty and slightly sweet. It can be finely diced and added to pasta, salads or shrimp dishes, and can also be used in sauces and drinks.

Ingredients

  • Lemon 4, 400g
  • Sea salt 440g
  • Sugar 440g
  • lemon juice 500g
  • Bay leaf 5g
  • Black peppercorns 5g

Instructions

  1. Mix lemon, sea salt and granulated sugar thoroughly.
  2. Add lemon juice, bay leaves and black peppercorns and mix well.
  3. Press the mixture tightly into a clean container.
  4. Refrigerate and marinate for 3 to 4 weeks.
  5. It is ready to use after marinating; the lemon peel will slowly soften.

Notes

  • The flavor of the finished product is sour, salty, slightly sweet, with the aroma of lemon peel.
  • It can be finely diced and added to pasta, salads or shrimp dishes, or used to make sauces or drinks.
