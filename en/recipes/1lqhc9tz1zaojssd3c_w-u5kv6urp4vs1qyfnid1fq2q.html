He Shangtai

🍋 Don’t waste the juiced green lemons. This time, I used 4.4 kg of seedless lemons left over from juicing in the store to make salted lemons. Instead of throwing it away, let it become a source of flavor for your next dish. Salted lemon recipe Lemon｜4,400 g sea salt｜440 g sugar｜440g lemon juice｜500 g bay leaf｜5g black peppercorns｜5g Mix lemon with salt and sugar thoroughly, then add lemon juice, bay leaf and black pepper, pack tightly into a clean container, refrigerate and marinate for 3 to 4 weeks. After pickling, the lemon peel will slowly become soft, sour, salty, slightly sweet, and will also have the beautiful aroma of the lemon peel itself. Afterwards, you can cut it into fine cubes and add it to pasta, salads, shrimp dishes, or use it to make sauces or mix drinks. I’m already looking forward to adding the taste of Sprite and Coke 👅 From the squeezed lemon to the next dish. It is very interesting in itself that the ingredients can be put to good use.

Ingredients

  • Lemon｜4,400 g
  • Sea salt｜440 g
  • Sugar｜440g
  • Lemon juice｜500 g
  • Bay leaf｜5g
  • Black peppercorns｜5g
  • This time I used 4.4kg leftover from the juice extraction process in the store.

Instructions

  1. Mix lemon with salt and sugar thoroughly.
  2. Add lemon juice, bay leaves and black pepper.
  3. Refrigerate and marinate for 3 to 4 weeks.
  4. After pickling, the lemon peel will slowly soften, becoming sour, salty, slightly sweet, and have a better aroma. You can then cut it into fine cubes and add it to pasta, salads, shrimp dishes, or use it to make sauces or drinks.
