Ingredients

  • 55g unsalted butter
  • 35g powdered sugar
  • 15g whole eggs
  • 100g soft flour
  • Almond poodle 20g
  • 1g salt
  • 45g unsalted butter
  • 45g powdered sugar
  • 45g whole eggs
  • Almond poodle 45g
  • Sweet chestnuts (Daiso) 200g
  • 120g milk
  • 40g sugar
  • 20-30g unsalted butter (room temperature)
  • 35% fresh cream 75g
  • 47% fresh cream 75g
  • 12g sugar
  • Sweet chestnuts to taste (for scattering before baking and for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180℃.
  2. Mix powdered sugar with room temperature butter and add 15g of whole eggs.
  3. Add the flour, almond powder, and salt and mix gently until there are no lumps.
  4. ジップロックに入れて、3mmの厚さにのばし、冷蔵庫で休ませる。
  5. モンブランクリームは耐熱ボウルに甘栗・牛乳・グラニュー糖を入れ、電子レンジ（600W）で1分温める。
  6. 温かいうちにバターを混ぜ、粗熱をとって、冷蔵庫に入れておく。
  7. Sprinkle with sweet chestnuts if you like, and bake in the oven at 160℃ for 25-35 minutes (do not air-bake).
