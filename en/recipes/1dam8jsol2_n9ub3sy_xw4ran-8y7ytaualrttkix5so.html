Sweet chestnut Mont Blanc tart

A tart filled with smooth Mont Blanc cream made from sweet chestnuts. Finish by layering crispy part sucre, damand, and chantilly.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • [Part Sucre] Unsalted butter 55g
  • 35g powdered sugar
  • 15g whole eggs
  • 100g soft flour
  • [Part Sucre] Almond Poodle 20g
  • 1g salt
  • [Damando] Unsalted butter 45g
  • 45g powdered sugar
  • 45g whole eggs
  • [Damando] Almond poodle 45g
  • [Mont Blanc Cream] Sweet chestnut (Daiso) 200g
  • 120g milk
  • 40g sugar
  • [Mont Blanc Cream] Unsalted butter (room temperature) 20-30g
  • [Chanti] 35% fresh cream 75g
  • [Chanti] 47% fresh cream 75g
  • 12g sugar
  • [Finishing] Sweet chestnuts, as desired (for scattering before baking and for decoration)

Instructions

  1. Make part sucre. Mix powdered sugar with room temperature unsalted butter and add 15g of whole eggs.
  2. Add the flour, almond powder, and salt and mix gently until there are no lumps.
  3. Place the dough in a ziplock bag, roll it out to a thickness of 3mm, and let it rest in the refrigerator.
  4. Make Damand.
  5. Make Mont Blanc cream. Place sweet chestnuts, milk, and granulated sugar in a heat-resistant bowl and heat in the microwave (600W) for 1 minute.
  6. Make a smooth paste using a hand blender and puree. Stir in unsalted butter while still warm, remove from heat and cool in the refrigerator.
  7. Make chantilly by combining 35% fresh cream, 47% fresh cream, and granulated sugar and whipping until 8/8.
  8. Place the cooled pât sucre into a No. 4 tart ring and squeeze in the damand.
  9. Sprinkle with sweet chestnuts if desired and bake in an oven at 160℃ for 25-35 minutes. Do not air-bake.
  10. Let the tart cool and pipe in the chantilly. Top with sweet chestnuts if you like and squeeze Chantilly again.
  11. Squeeze the Mont Blanc cream using a Mont Blanc nozzle and garnish with sweet chestnuts as you like.

Notes

  • Quantity is 1 size 4 tart ring.
  • Damand is all mixed in the same amount. If you don't have 45g of whole eggs, you can adjust the other ingredients according to the weight of the whole eggs.
  • If you add butter to the Mont Blanc cream, it will solidify when it cools, making it easier to maintain its squeezed shape.
