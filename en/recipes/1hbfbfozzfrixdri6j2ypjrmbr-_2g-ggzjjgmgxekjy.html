Formal Avocado Baked Brulee

Formal avocado bruschetta: avocado version of bruschetta One avocado Condensed milk The same weight as the avocado Milk 20ml Sugar Lemon juice 3-5ml Lemon zest Recipe by @juliettehenry__

Ingredients

  • avocado one
  • Condensed milk weighs the same as avocados
  • Milk 20ml
  • Lemon juice 3-5ml
  • Lemon peel appropriate amount
  • thin apricot
  • Pineapple juice 3-5ml
  • Pineapple skin appropriate amount
  • lemon juice 3 5ml
  • Recipe by @juliettehenry__

Instructions

  1. Cut the avocado and remove the seeds, keeping the skin as a container.
  2. Pour in the lemon juice and mix well, then add the lemon zest.
  3. Return the mixture to the avocado peel container that was not discarded.
  4. Grill until a classic crispy caramelized texture forms on the surface.
  5. Cut the thin apricots open and remove the seeds.
  6. Combine the thin apricots, boiled milk and sugar and stir with a machine or by hand until smooth.
  7. Add pineapple juice and pineapple skin, mix well.
  8. Place mixture back into thin apricot skin.
