Avocado Brulee

Baked brulee made with avocado, condensed milk and milk, with lemon aroma. The avocado skin can be reserved as a serving container and baked until a caramel crispy layer forms on the surface.

Ingredients

  • 1 avocado
  • Condensed milk weighs the same as avocado
  • Milk 20ml
  • Sugar in moderation
  • lemon juice 3–5ml
  • Lemon peel appropriate amount

Instructions

  1. Cut the avocado, remove the seeds, scoop out the pulp and keep the skin as a container.
  2. Mix avocado pulp with condensed milk, milk and sugar evenly.
  3. Add lemon juice and lemon zest and mix well.
  4. Pour mixture back into avocado crust.
  5. Bake until a classic caramel crispy layer forms on the surface.

Notes

  • The avocado peel should be reserved as a container for the pudding.
  • The recipe does not provide baking temperature and time, please adjust according to actual oven conditions.
