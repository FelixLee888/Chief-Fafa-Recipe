Formal Avocado Baked Brulee

Instructions

  1. Cut the avocado and remove the seeds, keeping the skin as a container.
  2. 淋入檸檬汁並拌勻，加入檸檬皮。
  3. Return the mixture to the avocado peel container that was not discarded.
  4. Grill until a classic crispy caramelized texture forms on the surface.
  5. 將薄杏切開並取出種子。
  6. 將薄杏、煮沸的牛奶和糖混合，用機器或手攪拌至均勻。
  7. Add pineapple juice and pineapple skin and mix well.
  8. 將混合物放回薄杏皮中。
