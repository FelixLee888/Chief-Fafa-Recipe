rice sticky skewers

It is a sweet and salty snack made by rolling freshly cooked rice with sugar to make it sticky, shaping it into skewers and baking it. Finish with a fragrant soy sauce-based sauce.

Ingredients

  • Freshly cooked rice: 1 regular bowl (according to the number of family members)
  • Sugar: 1 tablespoon (for mixing with rice)
  • Soy sauce: 2 tablespoons
  • Brown sugar (or regular sugar): Adjust to your desired sweetness
  • Chicken soup base: 1 teaspoon
  • White sesame: 1 tablespoon
  • Powdered chili pepper: 1 tablespoon
  • Sesame oil: 1 teaspoon
  • Salad oil: Appropriate amount (for grilling)

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, add soy sauce, brown sugar, chicken stock, white sesame seeds, chili powder, and sesame oil and mix well to make the sauce.
  2. Combine the freshly cooked rice and sugar and rub, pound, or knead until the rice and sugar are combined and the rice becomes chewy and sticky.
  3. Add a small amount of water to the rice bowl to prevent it from sticking, then transfer the rice.
  4. Apply a thin layer of sesame oil to the surface of the freezer bag. Place a spoonful of rice in the center of the bag and place a bamboo skewer in the center.
  5. Fold the bag in half, push out the rice, and shape it so that the bamboo skewer is in the center of the rice.
  6. Heat salad oil in a frying pan and place rice glutinous skewers over medium heat.
  7. Bake until the surface turns golden brown.
  8. Pour the sauce into the frying pan and use a brush or spoon to coat the rice mochi skewers evenly.
  9. Grill it a little more and it's done when the sauce is fragrant and coated.

Notes

  • Placing a bamboo skewer in the center of the rice will make it easier to shape it into a uniform skewer.
  • Adjust the sweetness of the sauce to your liking by adding brown sugar or regular sugar.
  • Apply a thin layer of sesame oil to the freezer bag to prevent rice from sticking.
