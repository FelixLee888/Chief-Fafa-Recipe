Butter-roasted rice cakes

This butter-roasted rice cake is creamy and less sweet. You can make about 12 pieces at a time. It tastes better after being baked and allowed to cool. If you can't finish it, you can keep it in the refrigerator.

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 40 min
Total: 50 min
Servings: 12顆

Ingredients

  • 1 room temperature egg
  • Sugar 40g
  • Condensed milk 25g
  • Honey 5g
  • salt 1g
  • Glutinous rice flour 132g
  • Corn flour 30g
  • Warm milk 200g
  • Melted butter 40g
  • Cream (appropriate amount to spread on the mold)

Instructions

  1. Mix eggs, sugar, condensed milk, honey, salt, glutinous rice flour, corn flour, warm milk and melted cream.
  2. Stir until there are no lumps of flour.
  3. Coat the mold with thick cream and pour the batter into the mold until it is about eight-quarters full.
  4. Preheat the oven to 180 degrees and bake the rice cake for 38 minutes.
  5. After baking, let cool before eating.

Notes

  • This recipe makes about 12 pieces.
  • If you can't finish it, you can keep it in the refrigerator.
  • After refrigeration, you can heat it in an air fryer at 180 degrees for 5 minutes for a better taste.
