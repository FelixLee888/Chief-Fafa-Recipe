Ingredients

  • 65g butter
  • 35g sugar
  • A pinch of Guérande salt
  • Rice flour (Kyoritsu Foods) 80g
  • Sweet chestnuts 100g
  • 20g sugar
  • 30g milk
  • Rum (dark) to taste
  • 1.5 sweet chestnuts
  • Fresh cream 50g
  • 2g sugar
  • 15-30g chopped sweet chestnuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bake the easy and delicious tart.
  2. Cut the sweet chestnuts for decoration in half and chop the remaining sweet chestnuts.
  3. Place sweet chestnuts, cane sugar, salt, and milk in a heat-resistant bowl.
  4. Cover with plastic wrap and heat for 30 seconds in a 500W microwave, stir once and heat for another 30 seconds.
  5. Place in a piping bag and chill in the refrigerator.
  6. Add the fresh cream and sugar and make into 9-minute pieces with firm peaks.
  7. Add chopped sweet chestnuts and mix.
  8. Squeeze the cooled marron cream and garnish with the sweet chestnuts cut in half.
  9. You can adjust the consistency of marron cream by adding milk little by little to make it easier to squeeze.
