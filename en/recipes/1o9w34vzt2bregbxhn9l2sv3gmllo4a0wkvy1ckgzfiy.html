Rice flour Mont Blanc tart

A gluten-free Mont Blanc tart made with sweet chestnuts available at Hyakuyen stores. A rice flour tart is layered with sweet chestnut marron cream and fresh cream with chopped sweet chestnuts.

Ingredients

  • 35g sugar
  • [Tart] A pinch of Guérande salt
  • [Tart] Rice flour (Kyoritsu Foods) 80g
  • 20g sugar
  • [Marron cream] A pinch of Guerande salt
  • 30g milk
  • [Marron Cream] Rum (dark) as desired
  • 1.5 sweet chestnuts
  • [Finishing] Fresh cream 50g
  • 2g sugar
  • [Finishing] 15-30g chopped sweet chestnuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bake the easy and delicious tart.
  2. Cut the sweet chestnuts for decoration in half and chop the remaining sweet chestnuts.
  3. Put the sweet chestnuts, cane sugar, salt, and milk for the marron cream into a heat-resistant bowl.
  4. Cover with plastic wrap and heat in a 500W microwave for 30 seconds. Stir once and heat for another 30 seconds.
  5. While still warm, make a paste using a blender or mixer. If squeezing with a Montblanc nozzle, strain it to prevent it from clogging.
  6. Add milk little by little and adjust to a smooth consistency. If adding rum, add the desired amount.
  7. Put the marron cream into a piping bag and chill it in the refrigerator.
  8. Add the fresh cream and sugar and make into 9-minute pieces with firm peaks.
  9. Add chopped sweet chestnuts and mix.
  10. Squeeze the sweet chestnut-infused fresh cream onto the baked tart and smooth the surface with a knife or palette knife.
  11. Squeeze the cooled marron cream and garnish with the sweet chestnuts cut in half.

Notes

  • Add the milk for the marron cream little by little and adjust to a consistency that is easy to squeeze.
  • Once the marron cream has cooled, squeeze it.
  • Adjust the amount of cane sugar depending on the sweetness of the sweet chestnuts.
  • If you use a Montblanc mouthpiece, puree the chestnut cream to prevent it from clogging.
