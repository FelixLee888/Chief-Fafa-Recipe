Coffee Jelly Noodles

Tender coffee jelly noodles are served in a sweet, creamy evaporated milk and vanilla broth. Top with coffee caviar for an additional coffee garnish.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: TBD
Total: 15 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 500 ml coffee
  • 5 g agar-agar powder
  • A few grams of brown sugar
  • Evaporated milk
  • Vanilla syrup, to taste

Instructions

  1. Whisk the agar-agar powder into the coffee.
  2. Add a few grams of brown sugar.
  3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour the mixture into a thin tray and allow it to cool until set.
  5. Fold the jelly and slice it into thin noodle-like strips.
  6. Combine evaporated milk and vanilla syrup to make the sweet, creamy broth.
  7. Serve the coffee jelly noodles in the broth.
  8. Top with coffee caviar, if using.

Notes

  • The source description lists 7.5 g agar-agar powder, while the ingredient record lists 5 g.
  • Using less agar-agar produces softer jelly noodles; using more produces firmer noodles.
