Earl Grey Soft-Center Basque Cheesecake

A creamy Earl Grey Basque cheesecake with a soft center and smooth, ice-cream-like texture. The recipe makes a 6-inch cheesecake.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 200g heavy cream
  • 4g Earl Grey tea leaves
  • 300g cream cheese, softened
  • 80g granulated sugar
  • 3 eggs, at room temperature
  • 10g cornstarch
  • 15g lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Combine the heavy cream and Earl Grey tea leaves in a mixing bowl.
  3. In another bowl, mix the softened cream cheese and granulated sugar until smooth.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Add the cornstarch and lemon juice, and mix until combined.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared 6-inch pan.
  7. Bake until the edges are set but the center remains soft.
  8. Cool before serving.

Notes

  • The cream cheese should be softened, and the eggs should be at room temperature.
  • The sugar can be reduced to taste.
  • The cheesecake is intended to have a soft center.
