Miso Orzo Pasta

A creamy five-ingredient orzo pasta made with white miso, Parmesan, butter, and shiitake mushrooms. Makes 4 portions.

Servings: ): 1. 45

Ingredients

  • 450g orzo
  • 85g unsalted butter
  • 45g white miso paste
  • 115g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 80g shiitake mushrooms
  • 400ml reserved pasta water

Instructions

  1. Remove the stalks from the shiitake mushrooms and cut the mushrooms into medium-sized slices.
  2. Melt the butter and fry the mushrooms until golden but still soft.
  3. Cook the orzo and reserve 400ml of the pasta water once it is cooked.
  4. Add the miso and 250ml of the reserved pasta water to the butter and mushrooms. Whisk until combined.
  5. Add the cooked orzo and grated Parmesan. Whisk until silky and creamy.
  6. If needed, gradually add more reserved pasta water until the desired consistency is reached.

Notes

  • Optionally garnish with togarashi or furikake.
