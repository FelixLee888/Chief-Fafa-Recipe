Classic Banana Cake

Ripe incense powder, added with oil and low-strength noodle flour, added with added nuts and added flavor. Easy to use, suitable for home baking.

Prep: TBD
Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Mature kasho 2 branches
  • 1 egg
  • 70g of oil
  • 50g nuts
  • 60g sugar
  • Low-strength noodle flour 150g
  • Foam powder 5g

Instructions

  1. Prepare one rectangular shape.
  2. Temperature softening and stirring of oil in the room.
  3. Add sugar, stir and mix until homogeneous.
  4. The temperature of the room is increased and the temperature is increased each time.
  5. Ripe aromatic powder, add low-strength noodle powder and foam powder after sifting, stir well.
  6. Ikiko joined nuts, mixed uniformity.
  7. The noodles are ready to be put into use, and the noodles are fully ripe.

Notes

  • Made using ripe incense sticks.
  • Circulation of oil and egg demand.
  • Kaikiko Natuka joins.
  • Due to the heat and temperature of the coming source, please adjust the situation of the fire box for use.
