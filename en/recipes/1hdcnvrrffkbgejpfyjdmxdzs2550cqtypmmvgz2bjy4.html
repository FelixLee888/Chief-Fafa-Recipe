Rich black tea pudding

A rich Earl Gray black tea pudding that doesn't require an oven and is made with egg yolk to give it a richer flavor. Equivalent to 5 145ml glasses.

Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 5g powdered gelatin
  • 30g water
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 40g sugar
  • 350g milk
  • 12g tea leaves
  • Fresh cream 130g
  • Fresh cream for finishing 70g
  • 5g sugar

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle powdered gelatin over cold water, mix well, and let stand in the refrigerator for about 15 minutes.
  2. Add milk and Earl Gray tea leaves to a small saucepan and stir over medium heat.
  3. When the center of the pot starts to bubble, turn off the heat, cover with plastic wrap, and let stand for 5 minutes.
  4. Add granulated sugar to the egg yolks and mix well.
  5. Strain the milk and tea leaves through a strainer to remove the tea leaves, add to the egg yolk and granulated sugar mixture, and mix well.
  6. Transfer the mixed batter to the small pot again and mix well while heating over low heat.
  7. Mix until the dough becomes thick. Even after you turn off the heat, the residual heat will continue to cook, so stir constantly with a rubber spatula.
  8. Remove the pot from the heat, heat the gelatin in a double boiler or microwave to melt it, then add it to the batter and mix well.
  9. Strain the dough again through a strainer and lower the temperature by submerging it in ice water.
  10. Once the mixture has cooled down, add 130g of fresh cream, mix well, and pour evenly into the glass.
  11. Chill in the refrigerator for 4 to 5 hours to harden.

Notes

  • Be careful not to burn yourself when heating milk or removing the plastic wrap.
  • If gelatin is heated too much, its coagulating power will be broken and it will not solidify, so as a guideline, heat it at 50-60℃.
  • The dough will be smooth at first, but will thicken as it cooks.
