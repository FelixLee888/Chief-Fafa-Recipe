Ingredients

  • 5g powdered gelatin
  • 30g water
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 40g sugar
  • 350g milk
  • 12g tea leaves
  • Fresh cream 130g
  • finishing cream
  • Fresh cream 70g
  • 5g sugar
  • Capacity 145ml

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle powdered gelatin over cold water, mix well, and leave to soften in the refrigerator for about 15 minutes.
  2. Combine milk and black tea leaves in a small pot and heat over medium heat.
  3. Add granulated sugar to the egg yolks and mix well.
  4. Strain ③ using a strainer to remove the tea leaves, then add to ④ and mix well.
  5. Transfer to a small pot again and mix well while heating over low heat.
  6. Mix well until it becomes thick.
  7. Remove from the heat, add the gelatin melted in step 1, and mix well.
  8. At this time, heat it in a hot water bath or in the microwave.
  9. Once the mixture has cooled down, add the fresh cream, mix well, pour evenly into the prepared glass, and refrigerate for 4 to 5 hours to solidify.
  10. Garnish with loosely whipped cream if you like, and it's done.
