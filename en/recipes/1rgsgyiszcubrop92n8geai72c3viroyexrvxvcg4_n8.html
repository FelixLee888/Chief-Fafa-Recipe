All-Purpose Lemon Cream Sauce

The tart and smooth lemon milk sauce is suitable for spreading on toast, serving as biscuits, sandwiching into cakes or as a cake topping. This milk sauce does not require fresh cream or gelatine and will become thicker when refrigerated.

Ingredients

  • lemon juice 90g
  • 2 lemon zest
  • Whole egg liquid 100g
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Fine sugar 50g
  • Unsalted butter 50g

Instructions

  1. Wash the lemon and peel off the zest, add fine sugar and knead it until fragrant.
  2. Add whole egg liquid, egg yolk and lemon juice, mix well.
  3. Place the mixture over simmering hot water and heat over a medium simmer, stirring constantly, until the sauce is thick enough to hold a spatula.
  4. Remove from heat, add unsalted butter cut into small pieces and stir until completely melted.
  5. Put the milk sauce into a clean container, seal it and put it in the refrigerator.

Notes

  • It is normal for the milk sauce to be liquidy just after cooking. It will become thicker and smoother after being refrigerated.
  • It is recommended to consume within 3 to 5 days after refrigeration.
  • Use a clean, dry spoon each time.
