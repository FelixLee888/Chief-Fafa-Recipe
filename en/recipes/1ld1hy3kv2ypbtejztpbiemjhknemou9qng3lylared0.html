Muscat flutter jelly and panna cotta

A dessert made by combining panna cotta with muscat and jelly cut into fluffy shapes using a 1.8cm diameter mold. Makes 4 glasses.

Ingredients

  • Muscat (appropriate amount)
  • 200g water
  • 60g sugar
  • 10g juice
  • Gelatin 10.5g (for jelly liquid)
  • 100g water
  • 100g milk
  • 10g sugar
  • 2.5g gelatin (for panna cotta)
  • 15g water
  • Fresh cream 50g

Instructions

  1. Let the jelly liquid solidify. Finish it off until firm, then use a fruit decorator with a diameter of 1.8cm to cut out flutter-shaped pieces.
  2. Arrange the muscats in 4 glasses, pour in the panna cotta liquid and let cool and harden.
  3. Arrange the fluttery jelly and muscat on the hardened panna cotta.

Notes

  • If the jelly is not hardened, it will be difficult to cut out cleanly.
  • If you arrange the muscats in a glass without any gaps, the muscats will not float easily even when you pour the jelly liquid.
  • If using blueberries, first pour enough jelly to just barely cover the blueberries so they don't float, then pour to the top the second time.
  • You can reduce the amount of jelly liquid used by filling the center of the glass with Muscat.
