Vegan Foie Gras

A creamy vegan pâté made from soaked cashews, sautéed shallots, mushrooms, garlic, cognac, and seasonings. The blended mixture sets in jars in the refrigerator.

Prep: ared mason jars and smooth
Cook: for about 10 minutes, removing the lid occasionally to stir
Total: 9 hr 35 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw cashews
  • 85 g coconut oil, divided
  • 90 g shallots, sliced
  • 50 g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 12 g fresh garlic, sliced
  • 110 g cognac or brandy
  • 275 g water
  • 25 g white miso paste
  • 15 g tomato paste
  • 8 g kosher salt
  • 5 g sweet spice
  • 5 g ground black pepper
  • 5 g low-acyl gellan gum F
  • 4 g high-acyl gellan gum LT100

Instructions

  1. Soak the cashews in water overnight. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat 15 g of the coconut oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the shallots, mushrooms, and garlic, stirring to coat them evenly and spread them in an even layer.
  3. Cover and cook for about 10 minutes, removing the lid occasionally to stir.
  4. Uncover the saucepan. If using a gas range, remove it from the burner before adding the cognac or brandy to avoid potential flare-ups. Add the liquor, return the pan to medium-high heat, and cook, stirring frequently, until the liquid is reduced by half, about 30 seconds to 1 minute.
  5. Add the drained cashews, water, remaining 70 g coconut oil, miso, tomato paste, salt, sweet spice, and black pepper. Stir well, bring to a boil, and cook for 30 seconds. Remove from the heat.
  6. Transfer the mixture to a blender jar. Remove the lid cap to allow steam to escape and cover the opening with a kitchen towel.
  7. Blend, starting on low speed and gradually increasing to high once a vortex forms. With the blender running on high, add both gellan gums and continue blending until completely smooth, about 2 minutes.
  8. Working quickly, divide the mixture evenly among prepared mason jars and smooth the surfaces.
  9. Place a small piece of plastic wrap directly on the surface in each jar to prevent a skin from forming. Refrigerate for about 1 hour, until set.
  10. Serve immediately once set, or cover the jars with airtight lids and refrigerate for up to 1 week.

Notes

  • Use caution when adding cognac or brandy near a gas flame.
  • Allow refrigerated pâté to sit for at least 20 minutes before serving.
