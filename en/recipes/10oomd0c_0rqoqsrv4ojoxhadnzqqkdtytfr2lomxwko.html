Ingredients

  • [Part Sucre] Unsalted butter 55g
  • 35g powdered sugar
  • 15g whole eggs
  • 100g soft flour
  • [Part Sucre] Almond Poodle 20g
  • [Damando] Unsalted butter 45g
  • 45g powdered sugar
  • 45g whole eggs
  • [Damando] Almond Poodle 45g
  • [Mont Blanc Cream] Sweet chestnut (Daiso) 200g
  • 120g milk
  • 40g sugar
  • [Mont Blanc Cream] Unsalted butter (room temperature) 20-30g
  • [Chanti] 35% fresh cream 75g
  • 12g sugar
  • [Others] Sweet chestnuts, as desired (sprinkle some before baking, for decoration)
  • 1g salt

Instructions

  1. Mix powdered sugar with room temperature butter and add 15g of whole eggs.
  2. Add the flour, almond powder, and salt and mix gently until there are no lumps.
  3. Place the dough in a ziplock bag, roll it out to a thickness of 3mm, and let it rest in the refrigerator.
  4. Sprinkle sweet chestnuts on the tart, bake, and garnish with Chantilly and Mont Blanc cream.
