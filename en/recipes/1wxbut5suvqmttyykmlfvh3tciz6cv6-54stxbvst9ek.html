Shai Muscat white wine jelly and panna cotta

A cool sweet made by layering smooth panna cotta on white wine jelly containing Shai muscat. You can also change the order of the white wine jelly and panna cotta.

Ingredients

  • Shy Muscat...appropriate amount
  • Water (for jelly liquid)...280cc
  • White wine...20cc
  • Lemon juice...1 tablespoon
  • Granulated sugar (for jelly liquid)...35g
  • Pururun Agar……10g
  • Milk...50cc
  • Fresh cream...100cc
  • Granulated sugar (for panna cotta)...15g
  • Gelatin...3g
  • Water (for gelatin)...15cc

Instructions

  1. Cut the shy muscat to your desired size and fill the glass up to the 8th mark.
  2. Put 280cc of water, white wine, and lemon juice in a pot.
  3. Combine agar and granulated sugar for the jelly liquid and mix well.
  4. Bring the liquid in the pot to a boil and add the agar and granulated sugar mixture. Stir well for about 1 minute while boiling, then turn off the heat.
  5. Once it has cooled down, pour it into a glass containing Shai Muscat and chill it in the refrigerator. Put the remaining jelly liquid in another container and let it solidify.
  6. Soak the gelatin in 15 cc of water.
  7. Place milk, cream, and granulated sugar for panna cotta in a pot and heat until just before boiling.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the softened gelatin and mix.
  9. Once the panna cotta mixture has cooled, pour it over the solidified white wine jelly and refrigerate.

Notes

  • Since it is an agar jelly liquid, you can also make it with fruits other than Shai Muscat.
  • Once the panna cotta has set, sprinkle with powdered sugar, then loosen the remaining agar and drizzle it with a spoon to create a dripping effect.
  • You can also change the amounts and the order in which they are layered, such as white wine jelly on top and panna cotta on the bottom.
