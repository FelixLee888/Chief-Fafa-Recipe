Ingredients

  • Shy Muscat...appropriate amount
  • Water...280cc
  • White wine...20cc
  • Lemon juice...1 tbsp
  • Granulated sugar...35g
  • Pururun Agar……10g
  • Milk...50cc
  • Fresh cream...100cc
  • Granulated sugar...15g
  • Gelatin...3g
  • Water...15cc

Instructions

  1. Place water, white wine, and lemon juice in a saucepan.
  2. Put it in a pot and bring it to a boil.
  3. Combine agar and granulated sugar and mix well.
  4. Add the agar and granulated sugar mixture to a boiling pot, stir well for about 1 minute while boiling, then turn off the heat.
  5. Put milk and cream in a pot, add granulated sugar and heat until just before boiling.
  6. Turn off the heat and stir in the gelatin and water.
