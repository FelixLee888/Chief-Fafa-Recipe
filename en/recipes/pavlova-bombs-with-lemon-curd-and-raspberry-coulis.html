Pavlova Bombs with Lemon Curd and Raspberry Coulis

Individual pavlova shells are filled with raspberry coulis, lemon curd, and whipped cream, then finished with pistachios, berries, mint, and icing sugar. The shells can be cooled overnight and stored in an airtight container until ready to fill.

Total: 13 hr 40 min
Servings: plates and place each pavlova on top, hole side

Ingredients

  • ml egg whites (from 3l
  • 2/3 cup caster sugar
  • 1 1/4 teaspoons cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon white vinegar
  • 250 g raspberries, fresh or frozen
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons white sugar, for the coulis
  • 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice, for the coulis
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar, for the curd
  • 1 tablespoon finely grated lemon zest
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice, for the curd
  • 50 g unsalted butter, cubed
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons white sugar, for the whipped cream
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Chopped pistachios, fresh raspberries, mint leaves, and icing sugar, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 225°F (110°C), or 210°C (100°C fan). Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  2. Beat the egg whites for 1 minute, until foamy. Gradually add the caster sugar over 30 seconds while continuing to beat, then beat for about 5 minutes, until thick and glossy.
  3. Add the cornstarch and white vinegar. Beat for another 30 seconds, until fully incorporated.
  4. Pipe 5 tall mounds onto the prepared tray and gently smooth them into neat domes.
  5. Bake for 1 1/2 hours. Turn off the oven and leave the pavlovas inside to cool completely for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  6. For the raspberry coulis, combine the raspberries, white sugar, and lemon juice in a small saucepan. Simmer for 3 minutes, mashing the fruit as it cooks.
  7. Blend the raspberry mixture into a smooth purée, then strain it through a sieve to remove the seeds. Chill until ready to use.
  8. For the lemon curd, rub the lemon zest into the sugar to release the oils. Whisk the egg yolks with the lemon sugar in a saucepan.
  9. Add the lemon juice and butter. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thick and custard-like.
  10. Strain the curd through a sieve, cover the surface with plastic wrap, and chill. Stir until smooth before using.
  11. Beat the heavy cream with the sugar and vanilla until softly whipped and fluffy.
  12. Place each pavlova in a muffin tin to hold it steady. Cut a small 1/2-inch hole in the base and gently push aside some of the soft marshmallow interior.
  13. Pipe in the raspberry coulis, followed by the lemon curd. Seal the hole with a little whipped cream.
  14. Pipe whipped cream onto serving plates and place each pavlova on top, hole side down.
  15. Finish with more whipped cream, chopped pistachios, a fresh raspberry, mint leaves, and a light dusting of icing sugar. Serve immediately.

Notes

  • Allow the pavlova shells to cool completely in the turned-off oven for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  • Store cooled pavlova shells in an airtight container until ready to use.
  • Chill the raspberry coulis and lemon curd before assembly.
  • Serve the assembled pavlova bombs immediately.
