Gorse Ice Cream

A smooth, creamy ice cream flavoured with the coconut-like scent of freshly picked gorse flowers. The recipe uses double cream, full-fat milk, egg yolks and caster sugar.

Prep: 10 min
Cook: TBD
Total: 48 hr 10 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 100 g freshly picked gorse flowers
  • 200 ml double cream
  • 150 ml full-fat milk
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 120 g caster sugar

Instructions

  1. Heat the gorse flowers, double cream and full-fat milk in a saucepan until warmed through.
  2. Meanwhile, beat the egg yolks and caster sugar together in a bowl.
  3. Sieve the warmed flower, cream and milk mixture into the bowl with the beaten egg yolks and sugar.
  4. Stir until all the sugar has dissolved.
  5. Pour the mixture into a container with a lid, cover and freeze for 48 hours.
  6. Serve the gorse ice cream.

Notes

  • Devon cream is recommended for the double cream.
  • Gorse flowers have a coconut-like scent.
  • The mixture is frozen for 48 hours in a lidded container.
