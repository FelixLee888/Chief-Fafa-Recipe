Vanilla Custard Puff Pastry Rolls

Flaky puff pastry rolls filled with silky vanilla custard, topped with sliced almonds and dusted with powdered sugar. Makes approximately 6–8 rolls.

Prep: 25 min
Cook: , stirring, until a thick custard for
Total: 45 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 400 g puff pastry, from the refrigerated section
  • ml milk ( 1cup
  • 1 egg yolk, for the custard
  • g sugar ( 3tbsp
  • g cornstarch ( 2tbsp
  • Some freshly ground vanilla, from a vanilla mill
  • 1 egg yolk, for brushing
  • 1 tsp milk, for brushing
  • Sliced almonds
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Instructions

  1. Heat the milk with the vanilla.
  2. Whisk together the custard egg yolk, sugar, and cornstarch.
  3. Gradually whisk in the warm milk.
  4. Return the mixture to the saucepan and cook, stirring, until a thick custard forms.
  5. Cover the custard directly with plastic wrap and let it cool completely.
  6. Cut the puff pastry into approximately 6–8 squares, each about 10 cm (4 inches) wide.
  7. Place a strip of vanilla custard near one edge of each square, leaving a small border.
  8. Roll up the pastry and place the rolls seam-side down on a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
  9. Whisk together the brushing egg yolk and milk, then brush the mixture over the rolls and sprinkle with sliced almonds.
  10. Bake at 200°C (390°F), using top and bottom heat, for about 18–22 minutes, until golden brown and flaky.
  11. Let the rolls cool completely.
  12. Lightly dust with powdered sugar before serving.

Notes

  • Cover the custard directly with plastic wrap while it cools.
  • The rolls are baked until golden brown and flaky.
