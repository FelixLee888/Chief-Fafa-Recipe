Sesame oil salted chicken

The chicken is marinated in sea salt with stir-fried spices, simmered, smoked as needed, and finally coated with sesame oil and cut into cubes. The finished product is salty and appetizing, with the aroma of sesame oil.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 hr 30 min
Total: 4 hr 45 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken
  • Sea salt 500g
  • sea salt 3 tablespoons
  • 1 slice cinnamon
  • 5 star anise
  • Geranium leaves appropriate amount
  • Sichuan peppercorns half tablespoon
  • 5 green onions (for fried salt, marinated chicken, and boiled chicken)
  • 3 pieces ginger
  • water 3.5 liters
  • Marathon Earth Brand Sesame Oil (MGB Extra Virgin Sesame Oil) appropriate amount
  • Rice 100g
  • sugar 40g
  • Tea 7.5g

Instructions

  1. Slowly stir-fry 500 grams of sea salt over medium heat until lightly browned. Add cinnamon, star anise and bay leaves and stir-fry until fragrant. Add half a tablespoon of Sichuan peppercorns and stir-fry until fragrant. Spread the fried spices and salt and let cool.
  2. Marinate the whole chicken with the fried spice salt and 3 tablespoons of sea salt. Place 1 piece of ginger and 2 green onions inside the chicken belly. Wrap it with plastic wrap and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours.
  3. Put the marinated chicken into 3.5 liters of water, add green onions and chopped ginger, and add about two tablespoons of fried spice salt.
  4. Bring the water to a boil again, then turn off the heat, cover the pot and simmer for 70 minutes. If the pot has poor heat retention, you can heat it over low heat for about 30 minutes, but do not let the water continue to boil.
  5. Use chopsticks to test whether the chicken is done; if there is still blood, continue steaming until done.
  6. Cut cooked chicken in half. To make the smoked version, combine rice, sugar and tea leaves, place in an old pot, set a steam rack inside the pot and place the chicken on top.
  7. Cover the pot and smoke for about 20 minutes, until the surface of the chicken is golden brown.
  8. Take out the chicken, apply a thin layer of sesame oil on the surface, and then cut into pieces such as chicken breasts, chicken legs and chicken wings.

Notes

  • The smoking step is an optional smoked version.
  • Use an old pot while smoking and place a rack inside the pot to support the chicken.
