Instructions

  1. Slowly stir-fry 500 grams of sea salt over medium heat until slightly brown, add 1 piece of cinnamon, 5 star anise, and appropriate amount of bay leaves, continue to stir-fry until fragrant, add half a tablespoon of Sichuan peppercorns, stir-fry until fragrant, spread out and cool.
  2. Spread another tablespoon of sea salt on the other side, stuff 1 piece of ginger and 2 green onions into the belly, wrap in plastic wrap and marinate in the refrigerator for 3 hours.
  3. After marinating, put the chicken into 3.5 liters of water, add scallions, ginger (crushed) and two tablespoons of previously fried fragrant salt. Boil the water until it boils, then turn off the heat and simmer with a lid for 70 minutes (if the pot has poor heat retention, you can heat it over low heat for 30 minutes but do not continue to boil the water).
  4. Use chopsticks to test whether the chicken is cooked through. If there is blood, steam it until it is cooked through.
  5. Cut the chicken in half and prepare the smoking ingredients: 100 grams of rice, 40 grams of sugar, and 7.5 grams of tea leaves. Mix evenly and put them in a pot for smoking. Place a rack in the pot, put the chicken on top, cover the pot and simmer for 20 minutes until the surface turns golden brown.
  6. Take out the smoked chicken, apply a thin layer of sesame oil on the surface, cut into pieces and divide into breasts, legs, and wings. Finish.
  7. Add 500g of sea salt until slightly yellow, add cinnamon, star anise and bay leaves, stir-fry until fragrant.
  8. Add half a teaspoon of Sichuan peppercorns and stir-fry again until fragrant.
  9. Add sesame oil to the fried salt to enhance the aroma.
  10. Marinate chicken, use sweet licorice seasoning, massage all over, add ginger and green onions.
  11. Wrap chicken in plastic wrap and marinate for at least three hours.
  12. Add the marinated chicken to 3.5 liters of water, scallions and ginger and simmer until the water boils, then turn off the heat.
  13. Cut the chicken in half, prepare the smoking ingredients and smoke for 20 minutes.
  14. When finished, cut the chicken legs, wings and breasts into cubes to create a delicious meal.
