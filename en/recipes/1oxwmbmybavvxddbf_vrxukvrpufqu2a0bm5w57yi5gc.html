Desktop macarons (beef cubes)

Taiwanese macarons (beef cubes): Taiwanese macarons/beef cubes (Taiwanese macarons, aka Niu Li) are so good, so plump, and totally irresistible. Every time I said I would only eat one, I ended up eating the entire pack. Slightly crispy on the outside, soft and tender on the inside, and paired with a sweet creamy filling, you really can’t stop eating it one bite after another! # Follow Eric Roll Your Eyes #Dessert # Taiwanese Snacks # Taiwanese Food # Afternoon Tea Recipe Shell: 2 eggs, 55g caster sugar, 60g low-gluten flour 60g, icing sugar (surface) 15g, filling: 80g unsalted butter, 30g icing sugar, 30g condensed milk 10g, 1 vanilla clip/vanilla extract 2ml, upper heat 200 degrees, lower heat 180 degrees, bake for 6 minutes

Prep: 10 min
Cook: 5 min
Total: 15 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Fine sugar 55g
  • Low gluten flour 60g
  • 15g powdered sugar
  • Unsalted butter 80g
  • 30g powdered sugar
  • Condensed milk 10g

Instructions

  1. Make a crust batter by mixing eggs, caster sugar and cake flour.
  2. Bake in the oven at 200 degrees and 180 degrees for 6 minutes.
  3. Make the cream filling by mixing unsalted butter, icing sugar, condensed milk and vanilla.
  4. Fill the baked shell with a creamy filling.
