carrot cake

The traditional radish cake made with radish, Chinese sausage, intestines and dried shrimps is made in a simple way. The ingredients are first mixed with rice flour into a slurry, and then steamed until the cake is firm.

Ingredients

  • Radish meat 1200g
  • 300g water
  • Salt 12g
  • sugar 20g
  • Chicken powder 12g
  • Crushed black pepper 5g
  • Rice flour 300g
  • 2 sausages
  • 2 pieces of intestines
  • Shrimp 60g
  • Lard 60g
  • 60g dried onion shreds

Instructions

  1. Wash the sausage and intestines and cut into strips.
  2. Peel the radish and cut into strips about the length of chopsticks.
  3. Mix the radish strips with Chinese sausage, sausage, dried shrimps, lard, shredded shallots, salt, sugar, chicken powder and black pepper.
  4. Mix the rice flour and 300 grams of water to form a slurry, add it to the radish ingredients, and stir until even.
  5. Put the mixed radish powder slurry into the steaming device and steam until the cake is firm.

Notes

  • Use fresh radishes and cut them into strips about the length of chopsticks.
