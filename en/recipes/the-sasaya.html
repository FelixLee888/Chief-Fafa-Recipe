A must-do if you have lemons: Lemon Milk Sauce! It’s really versatile! It’s delicious whether spread on toast, paired with biscuits, or sandwiched into cakes or used as cake topping👍 𝐩𝐬: This one made by Sha is a bit sour! No fresh cream or gelatin is needed. Heat it in water and refrigerate it and it will become thick and smooth~ It is a very simple and easy-to-make milk sauce that can be used every day! All-purpose lemon milk sauce: 90g lemon juice, 2 lemon zest, 100g whole egg liquid, 1 egg yolk, 50g caster sugar, 50g unsalted butter Wash and peel the lemon, rub it with sugar until fragrant, add whole egg liquid, egg yolk and lemon juice, stir evenly, place it over slightly boiling hot water and heat it over water, stirring continuously, heat until the milk sauce thickens and you can hang a spatula, then remove from the heat, add unsalted butter cut into small pieces, stir until completely melted, put it in a jar and keep it in the refrigerator. Sha's reminder ・It is normal for it to be runny just after cooking, and it will be thicker when refrigerated.・Put it into a clean container, seal it and refrigerate it. It is recommended to eat it within 3 to 5 days. Use a clean, dry spoon each time #lemonmilk sauce #lemon curd #handmade dessert #simple recipe #lemon

Ingredients

  • lemon juice 90g
  • 2 lemon zest
  • Whole egg liquid 100g
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Fine sugar 50g
  • Unsalted butter 50g

Instructions

  1. Add the whole egg liquid, egg yolk, and lemon juice and mix well.
  2. Place the mixture over slightly boiling hot water and heat over a pot of running water, stirring continuously during the process, until the milk sauce thickens and the spatula can be hung, then remove from the heat.
  3. Add unsalted butter cut into small pieces and stir until completely melted.
  4. It is normal for the food to be liquidy just after cooking. Please finish it within 3 to 5 days and use a clean, dry spoon each time.
