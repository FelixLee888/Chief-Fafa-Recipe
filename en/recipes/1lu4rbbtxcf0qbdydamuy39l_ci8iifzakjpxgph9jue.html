cotton cake

This is a cotton cake with a light texture and delicate surface. Follow the steps for the batter and bake with boiling water in the bottom to reduce defoaming and keep the cake moist.

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 1 hr 5 min
Total: 1 hr 20 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • 7 eggs
  • Approximately 60g each
  • Low gluten flour 105g
  • Corn oil 80g
  • Room temperature milk 100g
  • Lemon juice 7 grams
  • 70g white sugar

Instructions

  1. Separate egg yolks and whites. Sift the low-gluten flour, add corn oil, and stir until it reaches 70–80°C.
  2. Add room temperature milk and continue stirring until the batter is even.
  3. Add egg yolk and stir into a smooth and smooth egg yolk paste.
  4. Add lemon juice and sugar to the egg whites and beat until stiff peaks form.
  5. Take a portion of the meringue and mix it with the egg yolk paste, then pour it back into the remaining meringue, and stir gently until the batter is fine and smooth to avoid defoaming.
  6. Line a 28×28 cm square plate with oil paper or oilcloth, and pour the cake batter into the plate from a higher position.
  7. Preheat the oven to 150°C, place the square tray and bake for 65 minutes; adjust the temperature to 160°C for the last 10 minutes and add boiling water to the bottom of the oven.
  8. After taking it out of the oven, let it cool, peel off the oilcloth, trim the edges, and cut into any shape you like.

Notes

  • The egg whites need to be whipped until stiff peaks occur, and the movements should be gentle when stirring to avoid defoaming.
  • Use a 28×28 cm square pan and add boiling water to the bottom of the oven to bake.
  • After taking it out of the oven, let it cool before tearing off the oilcloth, trimming and cutting into pieces.
