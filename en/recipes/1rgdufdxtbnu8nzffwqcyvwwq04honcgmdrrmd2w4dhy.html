tea basque cheesecake

A tea-flavored Basque cheesecake flavored with fresh cream made from boiled Earl Gray and baked at 250℃. After firing, remove the rough heat and cool it overnight.

Ingredients

  • 200g fresh cream
  • 10g tea leaves
  • Cream cheese 350g
  • 100g sugar
  • Sour cream 90g
  • 12g rice flour

Instructions

  1. Line a 15cm mold with a removable bottom with parchment paper dampened with running water. Wipe off the moisture from the parchment paper before placing it in the mold.
  2. Place the fresh cream and Earl Gray tea leaves in a handheld pot and heat over low heat while stirring.
  3. Once it boils, turn off the heat and remove the tea leaves using a tea strainer.
  4. Soften the cream cheese. If using a microwave, heat at 500W for 10-20 seconds at a time, stirring with a rubber spatula each time, until the approximate temperature is 23-26℃.
  5. Add granulated sugar and knead with a rubber spatula until smooth.
  6. Add sour cream and mix. The recommended temperature is 15-18℃, and if you have just taken it out of the refrigerator, warm it up at 500W for 10-20 seconds before adding it.
  7. In a separate bowl, combine the whole eggs and egg yolks and mix without foaming. Place the tip of a whisk at the bottom of the bowl and mix gently. Boil the eggs in lukewarm water until the approximate temperature is 23-26℃.
  8. Add half of the egg mixture to the cheese mixture and mix gently with a rubber spatula attached to the bottom of the bowl.
  9. Add the remaining egg mixture and mix, making sure to brush around the bowl and rubber spatula.
  10. Add the boiled black tea and mix. The temperature of the dough should be approximately 40℃.
  11. Put the rice flour in a separate bowl, add some of the batter and mix well, then add to the whole batter and mix.
  12. Preheat the oven to 250℃.
  13. Strain the batter through a strainer and pour into the prepared mold. Gently tap the mold several times to remove air without spilling, and if there are small air bubbles on the surface, crush them with a bamboo skewer.
  14. Bake in a preheated oven at 250℃ for 25-27 minutes. Open the oven once after about 13 minutes of baking, flip the baking sheet over, and bake again to make it easier to bake evenly.
  15. Once baked, let it cool on a baking sheet. Once it has cooled down, put it in the refrigerator and let it cool overnight.
  16. Warm the knife in hot water of about 50℃, wipe it dry with a towel, and cut the cake. Wipe off the dough from the knife after each cut and repeat the process.

Notes

  • If you heat the cream cheese all at once, it will separate and affect the texture and browning, so heat it little by little and stir each time.
  • Mix the eggs gently to prevent them from becoming frothy and to avoid adding unnecessary air to the dough.
  • Fine-grained confectionery rice flour is recommended. Although weak flour can be substituted, rice flour is recommended.
  • Immediately after baking, the inside will be mushy, so be sure to remove the heat and let it cool overnight.
  • If the inside is still mushy even after chilling overnight, it may be under-baked, and it may be better next time if you extend the baking time by 2 to 6 minutes.
  • The baking time should be adjusted as a guide, as the heat power varies depending on the household oven.
