Ingredients

  • 5g powdered gelatin
  • 30g water
  • 40g sliced ​​almonds
  • 120g water
  • 400g milk
  • 50g sugar
  • Fresh cream 47% 100g
  • Matcha 8g
  • 15g sugar
  • 50g hot water

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle powdered gelatin over cold water, mix well, and leave to soften in the refrigerator for about 15 minutes.
  2. Add the almond liquid from ② and granulated sugar to a bowl containing 400g of milk, mix well, and heat at 600W for 4 minutes.
  3. Add melted gelatin at 500W for 30 seconds and mix well.
  4. Place in ice water and once cooled, add fresh cream (47% fat) and mix.
  5. Mix matcha and granulated sugar well.
  6. Add hot water and mix again.
