Blancmange with matcha sauce

This blancmange is made by chilling and solidifying the milk dough with the flavor of sliced almonds and finishing it with matcha sauce. Makes about 5 145ml glasses.

Prep: TBD
Cook: TBD
Total: TBD
Servings: 5個

Ingredients

  • 5g powdered gelatin
  • 30g water
  • 40g sliced almonds
  • 120g water
  • 400g milk
  • 50g sugar
  • Fresh cream (47% milk fat) 100g
  • Matcha 8g
  • 15g sugar
  • 50g hot water

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle powdered gelatin into 30g of cold water, mix well, and leave to soften in the refrigerator for about 15 minutes.
  2. Let the heated almond liquid sit until it has cooled down.
  3. Mix milk, almond liquid, and 50g of granulated sugar well and heat in a 600W microwave for 4 minutes.
  4. Heat the softened gelatin in a 500W microwave for 30 seconds to melt it, then add it to the milk liquid and mix well.
  5. Strain the dough through a strainer.
  6. Place in ice water to remove rough heat, add fresh cream and mix.
  7. Pour into your favorite glass and chill in the refrigerator for 7 to 8 hours.
  8. Mix matcha and 15g of granulated sugar well, add hot water and mix again.
  9. Strain the matcha sauce through a strainer and pour an appropriate amount over the hardened blancmange.

Notes

  • Sift the matcha in advance.
  • The used sliced almonds can be reused for toppings, baked goods, etc. by lightly draining the water, drying, and roasting them.
  • Spread the almonds on a cookie sheet and roast in the oven at 150℃ for 10-15 minutes, or in a frying pan over low heat until lightly browned.
