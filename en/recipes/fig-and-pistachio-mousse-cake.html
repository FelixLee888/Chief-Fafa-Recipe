Fig and Pistachio Mousse Cake

Fig and Pistachio Mousse Cake 🍂

Prep: 10 min
Cook: ies
Total: 8 hr 15 min
Servings: TBD

Ingredients

  • Amaretti crust
  • 200g amaretti cookies
  • 70g melted butter
  • Pistachio mousse
  • 200 g white chocolate
  • 300g pistachio spread
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 8 gelatin sheets
  • 12-14 figs

Instructions

  1. Slice 4 figs in half and quarter 2 figs.
  2. Crush the amaretti cookies, mix with melted butter and press into the bottom of the pan.
  3. Arrange the halved figs around the edges and place the whole figs in the centre. Fill the gaps with the remaining fig slices and let it cool in the fridge.
  4. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water for 5 minutes. Squeeze out the excess water, add to the cooled mixture and whisk until dissolved.
  5. Pour the cream mixture over the figs and refrigerate overnight.
  6. Decorate the top with fig slices and enjoy!
