Earl Grey Soft-Center Basque Cheesecake

材料

  • 200g heavy cream
  • 4g Earl Grey tea leaves
  • 300g cream cheese (softened)
  • 80g granulated sugar (can be reduced)
  • 3 eggs (room temperature)
  • 10g cornstarch
  • 15g lemon juice

作り方

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine heavy cream and Earl Grey tea leaves.
  3. In another bowl, mix softened cream cheese and granulated sugar until smooth.
  4. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
  6. Bake in the oven until set around the edges but soft in the center.
  7. Cool before serving.
