作り方

  1. Add the crushed Oreo 'soil' around the bottom of each stem while the Zephyr is still slightly sticky (poppy seeds also work great).
  2. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally, but don’t boil.
  3. Pour the hot milk mixture into the melted chocolate and stir until smooth.
